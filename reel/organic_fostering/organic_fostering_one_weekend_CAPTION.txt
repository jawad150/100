Fostering doesn't always mean every day. 💜

Respite fostering is planned care for a short period: a weekend, part of a school holiday, or another agreed time. It can support a child and their family, or give another foster household time to rest.

It works best when it's the same home, time after time. The time is short, but consistency matters.
