## What this video is

- **Concept.** One real month-end for T2, the reporting lead, in five beats: arrive, pull the export, check the number twice, call the client, log off. Two selfie beats (morning at the door, evening at the desk) bookend three phone b-roll beats. - **Hook.** Frame 1 is T2's selfie at the door, coat on, saying "Month-end." The real "25" tooltip sits as a small receipt in the corner by 0.8 s.
