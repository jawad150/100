Hulk Marketing: AI Video Concept

"Impossible to Miss" · 25 seconds · AI-generated (Kling AI or Seedance) · 9:16 · FOOH style

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • FOOH (fake out-of-home) ads are everywhere: giant products dropped into real city streets, things bursting out of billboards, a helicopter carrying a giant can. They look like real phone footage, which is why people stop and share them.
  • Cinematic commercials favour handheld, natural-looking shots, real human reactions, seamless transitions and motion blur.
  • Most FOOH is used by big consumer brands. Almost no marketing agency uses it to sell its own services, so that's our opening.

The Idea

What if a small local business was literally impossible to miss? A quiet UK high street, a small independent bakery that nobody notices. Then giant versions of Hulk's services appear in the real world around it: a huge map pin crashes down outside the door, giant five-star reviews float above the shop like balloons, the bakery's post bursts out of a billboard, and a bus rolls past with a giant "Book now" button. By the end there's a queue around the block. It's FOOH-style spectacle, and every giant object is a real Hulk service.

  • Map pin: SEO and Local Maps Marketing
  • Five-star reviews: Reputation Management
  • Billboard post: Social Media Marketing
  • "Book now" bus: PPC and Meta Ads

The Hook (first 2 seconds)

On-screen text: What if your business was impossible to miss?

Alternative hooks to test:

  • "POV: your business finally shows up on the map."
  • "This is what good marketing looks like from the street."

Who It's For

  • UK local business owners (cafés, bakeries, salons, shops, clinics, trades) who feel invisible next to bigger competitors.
  • Viewers who stop for eye-catching, shareable content: FOOH videos travel well on Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Look

  • Shot to look like real phone footage: handheld, natural daylight, slight shake, real UK high street (brick shopfronts, bus stop, pavement).
  • Giant objects in Hulk green (#9DEF69) with clean, glossy 3D finish so they read as the brand.
  • No real logos of Google, Meta or other companies: the map pin, stars and button are generic shapes.
  • Same bakery, same owner and the same street in every shot (make reference images first).

Shot by Shot

Shot 1 · 0:00–0:03 · Hook

Visual: Handheld shot of a quiet high street. A small bakery, nobody going in. The owner (a woman in her 40s in an apron) stands in the doorway, looking at the empty street.

On-screen text: What if your business was impossible to miss?

Sound: Quiet street ambience, a pigeon flutter.

Shot 2 · 0:03–0:07 · The map pin lands

Visual: A giant glossy green map pin drops from the sky and lands with a thud on the pavement right outside the bakery. Dust puffs up, the camera shakes, passers-by jump and then pull out their phones.

On-screen text: Found on the map.

Sound: A whistle of falling air, a heavy thud, gasps.

Shot 3 · 0:07–0:11 · Five-star reviews

Visual: Five giant green stars float up from behind the shop roof and hover over the bakery like balloons. A family stops and points up at them.

On-screen text: Trusted before they walk in.

Sound: Five soft chimes, one per star.

Shot 4 · 0:11–0:15 · The billboard

Visual: A billboard across the street shows a giant photo of a fresh croissant. A hand reaches out of the billboard in 3D and passes a real croissant to a surprised passer-by below.

On-screen text: Seen on every feed.

Sound: A pop as the hand breaks out of the billboard, a delighted laugh.

Shot 5 · 0:15–0:18 · The bus

Visual: A red double-decker bus drives past with a giant 3D green "Book now" button bulging out of its side. As it passes, the button presses itself and lets out a click.

On-screen text: In front of the right people.

Sound: Bus engine, a big satisfying click.

Shot 6 · 0:18–0:21 · The queue

Visual: Wide shot: a queue of customers now stretches from the bakery door down the street. The owner looks out of the window, stunned, then breaks into a big smile.

On-screen text: Impossible to miss.

Sound: Busy, happy street chatter, the shop door bell.

Shot 7 · 0:21–0:25 · End card (call to action)

Visual: The camera tilts up to the sky; the frame cuts to the forest green (#111A16) end card with the HULK MARKETING logo.

On-screen text: Make your business impossible to miss. · Get your free audit ↗ · hulkmarketing.co.uk

Sound: One final music hit.

AI Prompts (Kling AI or Seedance)

Create reference images first (the bakery shopfront, the owner and the street), then generate each shot as a 5-second image-to-video clip.

  1. Handheld phone footage of a quiet UK high street in daylight, a small independent bakery with a brick shopfront and nobody going in, a woman in her 40s in an apron standing in the doorway looking at the empty street, realistic, natural light, 9:16.
  2. A giant glossy bright green 3D map pin drops from the sky and lands heavily on the pavement outside a small bakery on a UK high street, dust puffs up, camera shakes, surprised passers-by jump back and take out their phones, realistic FOOH style, handheld.
  3. Five giant glossy green 3D stars float up from behind a bakery roof and hover in the sky above the shop like balloons, a family on the pavement stops and points up, realistic daylight, handheld phone footage.
  4. A billboard on a UK street shows a giant photo of a croissant, a hand reaches out of the billboard in 3D and passes a real croissant to a surprised passer-by below, realistic FOOH style, natural daylight.
  5. A red double-decker bus drives down a UK high street with a giant glossy green 3D button bulging out of its side, the button presses in as the bus passes, realistic FOOH style, handheld.
  6. Wide shot of a long queue of happy customers outside a small bakery on a UK high street, the owner in an apron looking out of the window and smiling, warm daylight, realistic.

Negative prompt: cartoon, real brand logos, readable text, distorted faces, extra fingers, warped buildings.

Production Notes

  • For the most realistic FOOH, start each clip from a real photo of a street (image-to-video) and let the AI add the giant object.
  • Add the "Book now" text and any lettering in the editor, not in the AI, so it stays sharp and spelled correctly.
  • Keep each shot to one action; Kling and Seedance handle simple, single movements best.
  • Add a small "Created with AI / visual effects" note in the caption, as platforms expect AI-made content to be labelled.
  • Captions on screen throughout for sound-off viewing.
  • Cutdown (15 seconds): Shots 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7.

Music and Sound

  • A playful, bouncy track that starts small and builds with each giant object.
  • Big, realistic impact sounds on every object (thud, chimes, pop, click) to sell the illusion.
