## What this video is

- **Concept.** On a wet afternoon between jobs, the owner of Leam Taxis is parked on a Leamington terraced street (at a kerb, never on a rank), engine off, reading the monthly report on his phone. A second phone propped in his dash mount films him. The reaction is small: he reads, scrolls back to check, breathes out a short laugh, maybe says one word to himself, rubs the back of his neck and looks out at the rain. It is labelled as a re-creation.
- **Hook.** A 1.5 s stitch of V03 (his "…twenty-five"), with the real September tooltip on screen by 0.8 s, then a hard cut into his car.
- **Why this brand.** Leam Taxis' Business Profile shows **25 calls in September 2026**, read exactly from the tooltip, the best of the six months shown. A September report is the one moment where that number lands, and V12 (T2 sending the report) and V03 (the owner's milestone) both lead into it.
- **What the edit reveals.** The real tooltip is keyed into the green screen of his phone, then pushed to full frame with its source line. The end card is the real headline-and-chart crop with its own range, `May–Oct 2026 (October part month)`. **Never** "best month since May", and never "114 calls since May" after a September report.
- **Best of all (plan):** he films the real moment himself next month, and we use that instead (Version B1 below).

- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings, such as a lounge or bedroom (not the kitchen). No TV, radio or music.
- Hold the phone about **30 cm** from your mouth (production_rules A.3, as in V03), slightly to one side so your breath doesn't pop.
- Use Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder), mono. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- Say it the way you'd say it to yourself, quietly, not as if you're reading it out.
