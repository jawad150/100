Technical SEO

Start with the things that can stop your pages being discovered or working well. We prioritise issues by their impact on your important pages.

  • Crawling & indexing: Check that important pages can be accessed and indexed.
  • Site structure: Review internal links, redirects and duplicate URLs.
  • Page experience: Identify speed and mobile usability improvements.

Keywords & search intent

Connect search demand with your actual services, locations and customer questions. The objective is relevance, not a long list of unrelated keywords.

  • Customer research: Map services and questions to the searches that matter.
  • Competitor gaps: Review opportunities where useful information is missing.
  • Page mapping: Give each important topic a suitable place on your site.

On-page optimisation

Improve the page a visitor lands on: what it says, how it is structured and where it takes them next. Search visibility and a useful customer experience should work together.

  • Titles & headings: Clarify the subject and purpose of each priority page.
  • Internal links: Help visitors explore relevant services and information.
  • Enquiry journey: Make the next action easy to find and understand.

Content & topic planning

Build and improve content around real questions, your expertise and what makes your offer useful. Clear, accurate information comes before word counts.

  • Content review: Identify outdated, thin or overlapping information.
  • Useful briefs: Plan service content and supporting topics with a purpose.
  • Expert input: Bring your experience, examples and approvals into the work.

Authority & relevant links

Look for relevant ways to make your expertise and business easier to discover. Outreach is about appropriate opportunities, not buying a promised position.

  • Link review: Understand the links already pointing to your website.
  • Relevant outreach: Identify suitable publishers, resources and relationships.
  • Useful assets: Develop information worth referencing and sharing.

Measurement & reporting

Look beyond a single ranking. Connect the work completed with available search, visitor and enquiry data, and be clear about what that data can and cannot show.

  • A starting point: Record agreed measures and their reporting periods.
  • Progress & context: Explain changes, tracking limits and work completed.
  • Next priorities: Make the next decisions clear rather than just send charts.

What happens in the free SEO audit?

We start with your website and your goals, then discuss the main areas worth investigating. The review is a starting conversation, not an automatic live scan. Any detailed audit, implementation and reporting scope is agreed before paid work starts.

How long does SEO take?

It depends on your starting point, competition and the changes involved. Some improvements may be reflected sooner than others; meaningful organic progress can take months. We set review points and explain the evidence rather than promise a fixed ranking date.

Can you guarantee a first-place Google ranking?

No. We do not control Google’s rankings and cannot guarantee a particular position. The focus is on relevant, useful work and clear reporting, not a promised number-one spot.

Do I need a new website first?

Not necessarily. We review whether your current website supports the work you need. Improvements to existing pages, content and technical foundations may be the right starting point. A redesign is discussed only where it is appropriate.

Are Google Ads and AI SEO included?

Google Ads management and advertising spend are separate unless expressly included in your proposal. AI-search content work depends on the selected SEO scope. The Authority SEO guide includes AI-search optimisation; no AI citation or placement is guaranteed.

What are the costs and contract terms?

The monthly guide rates are shown above. Your proposal needs to confirm the final scope, applicable VAT, any setup cost, minimum commitment and cancellation notice. There is no checkout or binding subscription in this design preview.
