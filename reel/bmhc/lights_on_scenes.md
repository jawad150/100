# BMHC — "Lights On" (people version) — scene plan

9:16 reel, about 45 s, from the "Lights On" concept PDF. One day in the 1843 building, Fajr to Isha. Every room appears the moment its light switches on, and this version shows the people who use each room.

Three devices run through the whole film:
- a day dial along the top (Fajr · Dhuhr · Asr · Maghrib · Isha) with the sun moving across it, turning to the moon at night
- a small building icon in the corner whose windows light up one by one as each room comes on
- a 3D object that pops on every room the voice names, with a light-switch click

Transitions are light-based: the screen flicks from dark to lit with a flare, warm light sweeps across, the camera pushes into a lit window, and blinds open. Captions are the voiceover word for word (Poppins Bold, white, key words in gold), with room names in ivory pills.

**Facts:** 1843, college, Grade II*, and the activities (daily prayers, Qur'an classes, GCSE Islamic Studies, taekwondo, Heritage Radio 24 hours a day, youth club, scouts, open days, Friday khutbah in English) all come from bmhc.org.uk, as listed in the PDF.

**Voice:** a warm, expressive British narrator who smiles on "still a place of learning!", the youth club and scouts, and "Help keep the lights on."

**Sound:** a light-switch click on every room, room tone, dawn birds at Fajr, a kettle at the open day and a soft studio hum. No music.

**Kept from BMHC's guidelines:**
- no depiction of the Prophet ﷺ
- Qur'ans shown only closed and cloth-covered on a stand
- no AI recitation or adhan
- no musical instruments on screen (the radio clip is cropped to leave out the guitars in the background)

**Changed from the PDF on request:** this version has people in it and uses an AI narrator. BMHC's PDF asks for empty rooms and a real voice from their team. Their voice recording can replace the narrator at any time.
