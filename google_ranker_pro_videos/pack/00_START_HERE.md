## 3. THE PIPELINE in 7 steps

1. **Consent and photos.** Get written consent covering likeness, voice, approved words, platforms, term and the right to withdraw. Then take **27 photos** with the rear camera, window daylight, a plain wall and no filters or Portrait mode (`research/production_rules.md` A.2). Add 3 front-camera selfies for anyone in a selfie clip (V03, V07, V09, V12, V13's cold open, V14). Record **voice memos**: each approved line separately, 3 takes, 5–8 s, numbers said as words, quiet room, phone about 30 cm away (A.3). Send full-size files, never over WhatsApp. For premises, add 6–10 wide photos.
2. **Character sheet.** Train a **Soul ID** on 20–30 photos and/or make a **Nano Banana Pro** sheet with the B.2 skeleton (3:2, 4k, count 4). Paste the person's wardrobe line verbatim (`pack/characters/`, or `master_plan.md` §3). **No face description, ever.** Build Hassan's sheet from fresh window-light photos, not his studio portraits.
3. **Crops.** Cut the sheet into single views: front head, three-quarter left, three-quarter right, full body front, hands. Attach those, **never the whole grid**.
4. **Anchor still.** **Generate the anchor still first, then animate.** Make one still per set-up with Soul 2.0 or Nano Banana Pro, at the deliverable ratio and in the capture mode's look. A shot needed in both ratios gets two stills. QC each still hard: skin pores, one light direction, no text, green screens showing all four corner dots.
5. **Seedance 2.5.** Paste the prompt from the video file. Attach the @Image references in the order the ASSET MAP lists them. @Audio is used only for a real person's own memo, under 30 s in total.
6. **QC.** Watch every clip at full resolution **with sound** before making the next. Use the checklist in `production_rules.md` §10. Change **one thing** per retry and log it. Never retry silently.
7. **Send back.** See §7 below.

> - **1080p, set explicitly every time.** It silently defaults lower. If only 720p is offered, use 720p and tell us; we upscale once in finishing.

## 4. REALISM RULES IN ONE BOX

> **The 10 rules that make it look like real footage**
> 1. **One named light source with falloff**, pasted verbatim (window, lamp, street lamp, overcast sky). Name where the shadow falls. No golden hour, no sun, no studio or ring light.
> 2. **One capture mode per video** (DOC-CAM, PHONE-SELFIE or PHONE-REAR). Its **texture line goes last, verbatim**. Never mix two modes in one clip.
> 3. **Atmosphere between lens and subject:** rain on glass, breath in cold air, steam from a mug, drizzle, a smeared windscreen.
> 4. **Lived-in dressing:** two or three anchors that stay put, plus **one uncomposed element** (a crooked blind, a cable on the floor, a half-cropped passer-by, a ticket face-down on the dash).
> 5. **Real physics and weight:** chairs creak, sleeves drag, tea shivers, suspension settles. Every car gets the VEHICLE lock.
> 6. **Behaviour, not emotion:** blinks, glances away, restarts a gesture, rubs the neck, a small nod. Never "looks happy" or "confident".
> 7. **The skin line:** real people get `Natural skin from the references, visible pores and fine texture, unretouched.`; anonymous hands get pores, fine hairs and one imperfection.
> 8. **Optics match the device:** phones have deep focus and no creamy bokeh; DOC-CAM has a gently soft background. The camera behaves like a hand, a shoulder or a tripod, with no gimbal, drone, dolly or slow motion.
> 9. **Strip the hype:** no cinematic, 8K, ultra-realistic, photorealistic, masterpiece, stunning, flawless, perfect, polished, professional, studio or glossy. No lens brands or ISO. No "no AI look"-style negations.
> 10. **One wet week in October:** paste the palette line for the scene (exterior, interior or car), keep colours muted, add **no readable text anywhere**, and make every screen turned away, soft or flat green with four corner dots.

> **If a clip looks like AI, CGI, plastic or a game render: fix in this order, one change per retry**
> - **Plastic or waxy skin:** strip any hype words → check the person has no face prose → check whether the anchor still was glossy and remake it → only then add `natural skin from the references, visible pores and fine texture, no smoothing.`
> - **Game render** (even light, everything sharp, too clean): name one light source with falloff → add two dressing anchors and one uncomposed element → add atmosphere → add physics lines → as a last resort, put `No game-engine look, no CG gloss.` next to the light line.
> - **Toy-like car:** VEHICLE lock → a real car photo as a prop reference → a shorter shot with the camera static while the car moves.
> - **Floaty motion:** add physics lines, use one action per stage and make the clip shorter. **Gimbal-smooth camera:** paste the handheld line and remove the move words.
> - Full table: `production_rules.md` D.7.

## 5. HONESTY IN ONE BOX

> - **Real people come only from their own photos, with written consent.** A generated face attached to a real name or a real quote is a fabricated testimonial, which the DMCC Act 2024 and the CAP Code ban. A face and a voice are also personal data. So: no consent, no clip. Never describe a real person's face, and never generate an "Ali".
> - **Client words:** we send questions, not sentences. The client answers in a voice memo, we transcribe it, they approve it, and only then do their exact words go into the prompt. Until then the prompt shows `[APPROVED LINE n FROM THEIR TRANSCRIPT]`. Owners say no numbers; the numbers are ours, on screen with a source. Agency people edit and approve our drafts.
> - **Disclosure labels** (on screen throughout; you and your adviser confirm the final wording):
>   - Real person, re-created: `AI re-creation made with [Name]'s permission. Their words, their voice. Real data.`
>   - Real voice over faceless footage: `Dramatised visuals. Real voice, real data.`
>   - AI presenter (fallback only): `AI presenter. Real client data. Not a client testimonial.`
>   - No-face b-roll: `Illustrative footage. Real data.` (V04: `Dramatised reconstruction · Real data`)
> - **Platform labels on every post:** TikTok AI-generated content (AIGC) toggle, YouTube "altered or synthetic content", Meta "AI info".
> - **No "No.1", "#1", "first", "just hit", "now", growth figures or up-arrows** until there is a dated ranking capture or a Search Console Compare export. 1.8 is said as "top two on average".
> - **Every number** comes from `assets/gsc/gsc_data.json` or a dated capture of a live site. Impressions are "times shown", never "people".
> - **This matches what your site already does:** googlerankerpro.com labels its presenters "AI-generated presenters and voices. Service explanations, not client testimonials."
