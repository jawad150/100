Hulk Marketing: Carousel Concept

"Don't Swipe" · 8 slides · 1080 × 1350 each (4:5) · Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • The most-saved agency carousels open with a curiosity cover that breaks a rule or teases a secret: "No one will read your carousel if…", "Why your customers don't remember you", "I'm this close…".
  • Seamless (panoramic) carousels are popular: one long design split across slides so an image or line continues as you swipe, which keeps people swiping.
  • Comparison slides ("Without SEO / With SEO", "boring vs viral") make value instantly clear.
  • Most agency carousels are cluttered tip lists with stock photos. A clever, minimal one stands out.

The Idea

Reverse psychology. The first slide tells people not to swipe. Of course, they swipe. Each slide teases them a little more, until the carousel reveals the point: you just did exactly what you want your customers to do. You kept going because something made you curious. Then it teaches three simple things that make customers keep going with a business, and ends with the free audit offer.

A single green line runs across every slide like a path (the seamless carousel trend). It starts as a small "stop" arrow on slide 1, keeps going when people swipe, and ends by leading straight into the CTA button on the last slide. The carousel proves Hulk understands attention by using attention on the viewer.

Design System

  • Background: forest #111A16 on every slide, so it feels like one continuous piece.
  • The green line: a thick Hulk green (#9DEF69) line, 14 px, that runs edge to edge and connects perfectly from slide to slide. Design all 8 slides as one long 8640 × 1350 artboard, then cut it into eight 1080 × 1350 slides.
  • Type: Inter Tight 900 for the big lines (110–160 px), Hanken Grotesk 400 for small supporting lines (minimum 34 px). Cream #F4F2E9 text.
  • One idea per slide: no more than about 12 words on any slide, lots of empty space.
  • Small slide counter in the top right (1/8, 2/8…) in muted cream; the Hulk logo only on the last slide.
  • No stock photos, icons or decoration. The line and the type do all the work.

Lead Capture

  • Organic: "Comment SWIPE and we'll send you the free audit link."
  • Paid: boost it as a Meta and LinkedIn carousel ad with a "Learn more" button to the free audit page.
  • Save-worthy: the three lessons give it value, so people save and share it, which boosts reach.

Why It Works

  • Scroll-stopping cover: "Don't swipe" is impossible to resist.
  • The seamless green line pulls people from slide to slide.
  • Simple and readable: one idea per slide, big type, no clutter, two fonts.
  • Useful: three clear lessons, not just self-promotion.
  • Conversion-focused: ends on one specific offer, the free audit.

Other Versions

  • Light version: cream background with ink text and the same green line, for a lighter grid.
  • Story teaser: slide 1 alone as a Story with "Don't tap the link" and a link sticker to the post.
