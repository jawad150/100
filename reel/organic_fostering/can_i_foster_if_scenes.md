# Organic Fostering — "Can I Foster If…?" (people version) — scene plan

9:16 reel, about 45–50 s. A real person lives each doubt. A glowing brand search bar floats beside them like a thought, and every word the narrator says pops its object on screen.
Brand colours: purple #B7006E, orange #FF6411, plum, ivory, lavender, peach, leaf. Type: Nunito.
Answer pills: Yes (leaf), Usually (green outline), Possibly (peach), Not automatically (lavender). Never red, never a cross.

Every answer card quotes organicfostering.co.uk/faqs word for word, as given in the concept PDF. A source tag sits on every card.

**Sound:** warm British male narrator who smiles on the "Yes!" answers and the CTA. Soft key ticks while the bar types, a pop per row, a click and ripple, a whoosh on each card, and a light, warm music bed ducked under the voice (the PDF allows either). Mastered at −14 LUFS.

**Note:** the PDF's guideline says "no people, no photos". This version uses people because it was asked for, and it keeps every other rule (honest answers, quotes only, no red or crosses).
