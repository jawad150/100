Our story

Built from experience. Created for ambitious UK businesses.

Google Ranker Pro began delivering digital growth internationally in 2012. In 2024, Hulk Marketing Ltd was established to bring that experience, talent and infrastructure directly to UK businesses.

Today, we help companies solve disconnected marketing, weak digital visibility and inefficient operations through one joined-up growth system. From SEO and paid media to websites, branding and AI automation, every discipline works towards the same commercial goal.

Hulk Marketing Ltd was incorporated on 2 September 2024 (company no. 15931335). Group figures above reflect Google Ranker Pro worldwide.
