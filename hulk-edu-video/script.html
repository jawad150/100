Hulk Marketing: Educational Video Series

"Fix It in 60" · 45–60 seconds per episode · AI-generated with Seedance or Kling · 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

The Idea

A weekly series of short, genuinely useful marketing lessons for small business owners. Each episode solves one common problem in under a minute: a Hulk specialist explains it to camera, the edit shows the fix on screen, and the viewer can do it themselves today.

It's the same educational style as the earlier SEO lesson video (V07): one person, one clear lesson, real proof on screen. It builds trust first and sells second. People save and share useful tips, and the business owners who don't have time to do it themselves book the free audit.

Series Format (every episode)

  1. Hook (0–3 s): the problem in one line, said straight to camera, with a bold caption.
  2. Why it matters (3–10 s): one sentence on what it costs the business.
  3. The fix (10–45 s): three numbered steps, each shown on screen with a simple mock-up or a real screen recording.
  4. Recap and CTA (45–60 s): "Save this for later. Want us to check yours? Get your free audit."

Series look: the presenter in a real-looking modern office or walking outside. Hulk forest green and cream caption bars, big numbered steps ("01", "02", "03") in Hulk green (#9DEF69), the Inter Tight and Hanken Grotesk fonts, and a small "Fix It in 60" series badge in the corner.

Seedance / Kling Prompts (Episode 1)

Generate the presenter from a character reference image (or, better, from photos of a real Hulk team member who has agreed to appear). Leave all phone and screen content blank; it is added in the edit.

  1. Hook: A friendly marketing specialist in their 30s in smart casual clothes walks towards the camera in a bright modern UK office, holding up a smartphone with a blank screen, talking directly to camera with natural hand gestures, handheld phone-camera look, soft daylight, realistic, 9:16.
  2. Talking close-up: The same person in a medium close-up, sitting on the edge of a desk, explaining something to camera with a warm, confident expression and natural gestures, shallow depth of field, soft window light, realistic.
  3. Recap: The same person smiles at the camera and gives a small nod, then points down towards the bottom of the frame, bright office background, realistic.

Negative prompt: cartoon, readable text, logos, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped phone, mouth artefacts.

Voice and Presenter

  • Best option: a real Hulk specialist records the voice-over (quiet room, phone 30 cm away, one line per take), and the AI video is generated from their photos with their written consent. This keeps it honest and on-brand.
  • If no team member can appear: use a clearly fictional AI presenter and label the video "AI presenter" in the corner and the caption. Never present an AI person as a real Hulk employee.
  • Advice must be general and true. No invented statistics, rankings or results; if a real client's data is shown, get their written permission first.

More Episode Ideas (same format)

  2. "Stop boosting posts": three reasons a proper Meta ad campaign beats the "Boost" button, and when boosting is fine.
  3. "Write a Google review reply in 30 seconds": a simple three-part template for good and bad reviews.
  4. "Is your website slow?": how to test it for free and the three most common fixes.
  5. "3 jobs AI can do for your business this week": instant enquiry replies, review summaries and first drafts of social posts.
  6. "One keyword, four searches": why "taxi Warwick" and "Warwick taxis" can perform differently, and how to check yours (with client permission for any real data shown).

Production Notes

  • Captions on screen throughout, large and inside the safe area (clear of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px).
  • All screen mock-ups are generic (no Google, Meta or other real logos) and built in the edit, not by the AI.
  • One lesson per episode; never more than three steps.
  • Post weekly on the same day so it becomes a recognisable series; pin the first episode to the profile.
  • Lead capture: "Comment AUDIT and we'll check your profile for free", plus a free audit link in bio and in paid versions.
