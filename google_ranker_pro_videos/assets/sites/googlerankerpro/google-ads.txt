 BEFORE WE START
 Good questions.
 Clear answers . 
 No jargon or vague promises. Just the practical details you need to decide.
 Ask us a question 
 Can you help if I have never run Google Ads? Yes. Start by telling us what you offer, the areas or audiences you want to reach and the action you want customers to take. We can then discuss campaign planning, website readiness, tracking and a suitable starting scope.
 Can you review my existing campaigns? Yes. The initial conversation helps establish your goals and concerns. Any account access needed for a deeper review is arranged through account permissions, not by asking you to share your password.
 How much advertising budget do I need? There is no single figure that fits every business. The starting point depends on the market, locations, services and competition, together with your commercial goals. Advertising spend is discussed separately from our management fee.
 How quickly will I get leads? Timing and results depend on campaign approval, demand, targeting, competition, budget, website experience and measurement. We do not guarantee leads, a particular cost per enquiry or a fixed return. We agree the review process and explain what the available data shows.
 Will you build a landing page for my campaign? We can review the landing experience as part of the agreed scope. A new landing page or a larger website change is scoped and quoted separately unless your proposal explicitly includes it.
 Does Google Ads improve my organic SEO rankings? Paid advertising and organic SEO are different channels. Paying for adverts does not buy better organic rankings. Google Ads can be considered alongside SEO, with each channel measured for its own purpose.
 What happens after I send an enquiry? Your details help us understand the service, website and campaign context. The next step is a conversation about priorities, required access and the proposed scope. This design preview does not send enquiries until its submission service is connected.
