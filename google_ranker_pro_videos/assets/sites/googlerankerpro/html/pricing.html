BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Understand the scope, the costs and the next step before committing to a plan.

Are these the final prices?

These are the monthly figures in our 2026 pricing guide, shown here for design review. VAT treatment, setup fees, minimum terms and final scope still need confirmation. A written proposal will state the full payable amount before any commitment.

Which plan is right for my business?

Local SEO focuses on discovery in a local market. Growth SEO covers a broader organic programme. Authority SEO extends the Growth scope for more demanding markets. Your website, service area, competition and priorities help determine the right fit.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The pricing guide refers to a three-month SEO minimum and also to 30 days’ cancellation notice. The initial period, renewal arrangements and notice requirements must be confirmed together in the final proposal. This preview does not create a subscription.

Do these fees include advertising spend?

These are SEO plans, not paid advertising budgets. Google Ads and Meta Ads require a separately agreed scope, management fee and media budget. No ad-management price is assumed in the figures above.

Is AI SEO included?

The Authority SEO guide includes AI Overviews / AEO optimisation. Any AI SEO work outside that scope needs to be agreed separately. No package promises rankings or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.

Can we start with an audit?

Yes. Use the audit enquiry to share your website and priorities before choosing a plan. In this design preview, the form prepares an unsent draft rather than submitting your details.
