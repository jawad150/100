## Respect rules (from BMHC's concept PDF)
- No depiction of the Prophet ﷺ, angels or Companions. The people shown are today's community and stock footage.
- Verses appear as the English translation (M.A.S. Abdel Haleem) with the reference. Qur'anic Arabic is not typed, generated or animated. If wanted, verified Uthmani text, approved by BMHC's imam, can be added.
- No AI recitation. If a recitation is wanted, it should be recorded by a qualified reciter.
- No music, and no sound effects under the verse.
- Every fact carries a source tag.

## Sound plan
- Expressive, smiling male English narrator (local TTS) with warmer, brighter delivery on the hook, "Iqra" and the CTA.
- No music, per the brief.
- Soft UI pops and whooshes on the pop-ups, silent through the verse beats.
- Gentle fade on the last word.
