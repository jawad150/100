## What this video is

- **Concept.** At the end of a night shift the owner of Leam Taxis walks from his parked car to his front door, filming himself on his phone's front camera, and says the number in his own words: a hundred and fourteen calls off his Google listing, May to October. At his door he adds the best month: September, twenty-five. Then he turns the key.
- **Hook.** Frame 1 is already his face mid-step, with two short buzzes from a phone in his pocket. The real `114` headline crop is on screen by 0.8 s with its source line, and a 0.6 s flash of his hand slamming the car door gives the first beat.
- **Why this brand.** A running total is the one milestone this data proves without a comparison period: 114 calls made from the Business Profile, May–Oct 2026 (October part month), with September the best of the six months shown at 25 (read exactly from the tooltip). A taxi owner would say that number out loud. It also sets up V09's stitch ("…twenty-five").
- **What the edit reveals.** The real headline crop lands first. The real chart then wipes on left to right above him as he walks (the real pixels revealed, never redrawn). On his word "twenty-five" we punch in on the real September tooltip with the caption `September: 25 calls, the best of the six months shown.` No growth claims, no "first", no "now".

**How to record a memo (send this to him):**
- **Only after G3 clears.**
- One approved line per memo: three takes in the same file, with a 2-second gap between takes. Each take is 5–8 s at most.
- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings, such as a lounge or bedroom (not the kitchen or the car). No TV, radio or music.
- Hold the phone about **30 cm** from your mouth (production_rules A.3), slightly to one side so your breath doesn't pop.
- Use Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder), mono. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- Say it the way you'd tell a mate, not as if you're reading it out.
- **Then record both lines a second time outdoors**, on your phone at arm's length while walking slowly on your street, and save them as `MEMO_CLIENT_LEAM_OWNER_v03_line1_outdoor.m4a` and `..._line2_outdoor.m4a`. These give us the real phone-microphone sound for the final track.
- Send full-size files by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Not WhatsApp.**
- Nobody's accent is ever described in a prompt. The memo carries the voice.

**Which memo goes where:** the clean indoor take is the `@Audio 1` reference in Seedance, because it guides timbre and pace best. In the edit we lay in his real voice (`_outdoor` first, or the indoor take with a light phone-mic EQ) wherever sync allows. **If the generated voice doesn't sound like him, that is a QC fail** (production_rules G.1). Never ship a voice he hasn't heard and approved.
