Hulk Marketing: AI Video Concept

"The Night Shift" · 30 seconds · AI-generated (Kling AI or Seedance) · 9:16 · Miniature workers style

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • "Mini workers" AI videos are a fast-growing trend: tiny construction workers in hard hats building, repairing or cooking on everyday objects, filmed in close-up macro with a shallow depth of field.
  • Pins show tiny crews on food, inside phones and on circuit boards, using cranes, ladders, wheelbarrows and scaffolding. They're satisfying to watch and get shared widely.
  • Food and product brands use it; marketing agencies hardly do. Using it to show the work behind marketing is a fresh angle.

The Idea

It's late at night and a business owner has gone home, leaving the laptop and phone on the desk. Then a tiny Hulk crew in green hard hats arrives and works the night shift. They build the website brick by brick on the laptop screen, use a crane to lift five gold stars onto the phone, hoist the business's name to the top of a search list, paint a mini billboard and feed a pile of paperwork into a tiny automation machine. In the morning, the owner comes in with a coffee and the phone starts buzzing with new enquiries. One tiny worker tips his hat and slips away behind the mug.

Each job is one of Hulk's real services:

  • Building the website on the laptop: Website Design and Development
  • Craning stars onto the phone: Reputation Management
  • Hoisting the name to the top of a search list: SEO
  • Painting a mini billboard: Social Media and Paid Ads
  • The paperwork machine: AI Automation

The Hook (first 2 seconds)

On-screen text: While you were sleeping, a tiny team was building your marketing.

Alternative hooks to test:

  • "Ever wondered who's behind great marketing?"
  • "Small team. Massive results." (the crew looks tiny; the impact looks big)

Who It's For

  • Busy UK small business owners who don't have time to do their own marketing.
  • The message: hand it to Hulk and the work gets done while you run your business.

Look

  • Macro, tilt-shift miniature look: very shallow depth of field, warm desk-lamp light at night, cool morning daylight at the end.
  • The tiny workers wear green (#9DEF69) hard hats with a small "H" and dark green work overalls, so they're instantly the Hulk crew.
  • Realistic textures on everyday objects (laptop keys, phone glass, coffee mug, notepad).
  • All screens (website, reviews, search list, notifications) are mock-ups added in editing, so the text stays sharp and no real platform logos appear.

  2. Macro shot of tiny miniature construction workers in green hard hats laying glowing rectangular blocks like bricks on a laptop screen, building a web page, one worker with a paint roller, warm lamp light, shallow depth of field, realistic.
  3. Macro shot of a tiny construction crane on a desk lifting a golden star and lowering it onto a smartphone screen, tiny workers in green hard hats guiding it into place, warm lamp light, realistic miniature.
  4. Macro shot of tiny workers in green hard hats pulling a block up a ladder with a rope to the top of a paper list propped up on a desk, teamwork, warm lamp light, realistic miniature.
  5. Macro shot of tiny workers in green hard hats on scaffolding painting a small billboard standing on a desk, green paint, warm lamp light, realistic miniature.
  6. Macro shot of a tiny worker in a green hard hat shovelling messy sticky notes into a small green machine, neat folders coming out on a tiny conveyor belt, warm lamp light, realistic miniature.

Negative prompt: cartoon, readable text, real brand logos, distorted faces, extra limbs, blurry workers.

Production Notes

  • Keep the same worker reference image in every shot so the crew looks consistent.
  • Add all screen content (website, stars, search list, notifications) and the lettering on the billboard in the editor.
  • One action per shot; mini-worker clips work best as short, satisfying moments.
  • Add a small "Created with AI" note in the caption.
  • Captions on screen throughout for sound-off viewing.
  • Cutdown (15 seconds): Shots 1, 2, 3, 7 and 8.

Music and Sound

  • A light, playful, tiptoeing track at night that becomes bright and upbeat in the morning.
  • Lots of tiny, detailed sound effects (hammers, cranes, clinks, whistles); they make miniature videos so satisfying.
