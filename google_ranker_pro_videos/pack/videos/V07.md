## What this video is

- **Concept.** T1, the agency's SEO specialist, walks out of their real workplace in drizzle and tells their phone the most useful thing they learned this month. On the same taxi site, over the same three months, four ways of typing "Kenilworth taxi" sat at four different average positions: 6.7, 16.4, 8.6 and 8.5. Tiny wording differences behave like separate searches, so you check every variant.
- **Hook.** The real row 4 receipt (`kenilworth taxis · 6.7`) is on screen by 0.5 s, and row 13 (`kenilworth taxi · 16.4`) snaps in under it at 0.8 s. T1 is already mid-step on the front camera.
- **Why this brand.** Golden Line is the client with a verified Hulk footer credit and an attributed Search Console screenshot. These four rows are real, sit in one export, and need no comparison period. The lesson is true without any growth claim.
- **What the edit reveals.** As T1 says each number, we punch in on their face and the real Search Console row for that wording drops into the upper band. Four real receipts end up stacked, with the gap between 6.7 and 16.4 plain to see. A 1.4 s real Kenilworth clip (RF03) sits under "sixteen point four" and hides the join between the two takes.

**Changes from the plan (one line each):**
- **Re-grip moved** from second 6 to second 11 in `v07_A-01`. Second 6 is where line 2 starts, and a camera jolt during speech causes mouth mush (production_rules D.7).
- **VEHICLE lock added** to `v07_A-02`, where cars pass and stop in frame; §2.10 puts the lock in every prompt with a car exterior. `v07_A-01` has no car in frame, so it has no lock (a parked car added a plate risk and nothing to the story).
- **Bus shelter:** the plan's "unreadable advert" becomes a shelter **seen from its back, its advert panel turned away so only its plain grey metal back shows**. An advert face is the strongest text magnet on a street.
- **Crossing:** a signal-controlled crossing, because zebra crossings don't beep. Its push-button box (it carries a "WAIT" panel) is turned away from the lens.
- **Framing:** the head sits in the lower-middle of the frame so the four receipts fit the upper band inside the safe zone. The selfie line "top of the head nearly touching the top of the frame" is therefore not used.
- **Receipts:** a full Search Console row (1318 × 49 px) is unreadable at phone width. Each receipt therefore shows the row's **real query cell and real position cell side by side, with a visible gap**: two exact crops of the same row, nothing re-typed. The full-width rows appear on the post's cover frame. We use the §1.2 orange **underline**, not the plan's "ring", so V07 matches the series.
- **Light:** L-STREET is pasted verbatim, plus one sentence per shot naming which side of the face is brighter. A flat overcast line on its own invites the even, shadowless "render" look. In the hands-only fallback `P-01`, its "soft shadows under the eyes and chin" becomes "soft shadows under the hands and the phone", because no face is in frame (as V13's H-01 does).
- **No breath vapour.** It is a daytime shot at around 10 °C. The atmosphere is drizzle instead.
- **Fallback:** given the ID `v07_P-01_9x16` (the plan names the shot but gives no ID). The thumb types over the lower third of the green screen, which relaxes SCREEN-GREEN's "one brief tap", because typing is the action. The four corner dots stay clear, and we key around the thumb.
- **Memo distance:** about 30 cm, as in production_rules A.3 and T1's character file, so every memo in the set is recorded the same way.
- **A-02 ends on the turn, not the step off the kerb.** The last second held the beep, a glance back, the laugh, a full turn and a step, which breaks one-change-per-stage and smears the face in the second the end card uses. It now holds the beep, the laugh and the start of the turn. The cut ends at 20.0 s (clip 9.0–9.9 s).
