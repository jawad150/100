Hulk Marketing: Carousel Concept

"The Leaky Pipe" · 7 slides · 1080 × 1350 each (4:5) · seamless (one continuous illustration) · Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • The most-saved agency carousels open with a problem the viewer feels personally ("Why your customers don't remember you").
  • Seamless carousels, where one illustration continues across every slide, keep people swiping to see where it goes.
  • "Without / with" comparisons make the value obvious in one look.
  • Most agency carousels are cluttered tip lists with stock photos; a single bold illustration stands out.

The Idea

Every business has a pipeline: people looking for what it sells at one end, paying customers at the other. Most pipelines leak. This carousel draws that pipeline as one long glass pipe running across every slide, full of green drops (customers). As the viewer swipes, they watch drops leak out at three cracks: they can't find you, your website loses them, nobody follows up. By slide 5 only a trickle reaches the glass. Then Hulk seals the leaks, and the pipe fills a bucket to overflowing.

The viewer literally follows the customer journey with their thumb, and each leak is a real Hulk service.

Why It Stops the Scroll

  • The cover asks a scary, personal question: "Your marketing is leaking customers."
  • The pipe runs off the edge of every slide, so people have to swipe to see where it goes.
  • One clear illustration, big type, one idea per slide, two fonts, no stock photos.
  • It ends with a direct, relevant offer: "Find your leaks" = the free audit.

Design System

  • Forest #111A16 background; cream #F4F2E9 pipe and text; Hulk green #9DEF69 drops, patches and button.
  • Inter Tight 900 headlines (104–132 px), Hanken Grotesk for small text (minimum 34 px).
  • Built as one 7560 × 1350 artboard and cut into seven 1080 × 1350 slides, so the pipe lines up perfectly.
  • Slide counter (1/7…) top right; Hulk logo only on the last slide.

The Full Carousel (one continuous illustration)

Slides 1–5 show the leaking pipe as one continuous line. Slides 6–7 show the fixed pipe pouring into a full bucket.

1/7 · Cover (hook)

2/7 · Leak 01

Headline: They can't find you.

Small text: Invisible on Google and the map, so they pick whoever shows up first.

Visual: The first crack: drops spill out of the pipe into a green puddle (SEO and Local Maps).

3/7 · Leak 02

4/7 · Leak 03

Headline: Nobody follows up.

Small text: Enquiries sit in the inbox until they've gone elsewhere.

Visual: The pipe ends at a tap that drips into an almost empty glass.

Visual: A new pipe, full of drops, with three green Hulk patches labelled SEO & Maps, Website and AI follow-up.
