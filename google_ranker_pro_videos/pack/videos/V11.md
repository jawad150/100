## What this video is

- **Concept.** An honest look behind the scenes. Every case study starts with no numbers, just a site map. The hands lead: a marker squeaks out an empty box and an arrow, blank Post-its go onto a monitor, and blank index cards are laid out like a family tree beside a green laptop. Then one medium-wide two-shot of T1 and T2 at the whiteboard: T2 draws, T1 points at a gap, a low wordless murmur, one laugh, and the kettle clicks off next door.
- **Hook (0–0.8 s).** The text card `Day one. No numbers yet.` is on screen from frame 1, over the first marker squeak.
- **Why this brand.** Zentro is not a taxi firm, so it widens the agency's story. It also has **no performance data on file at all**: no Search Console, no Business Profile, no rankings. That is exactly what "day one" looks like. The only real thing we can show is the structure of the site, and that structure is real: **10 laptop brands and 3 phone brands, each with its own repair page.**
- **What the edit reveals.** The green laptop gets Zentro's real brand-repair page, keyed in from today's capture. We push into the screen, and the real brand grids fill the frame. Each brand tile is outlined one by one, then the card reads `10 laptop brands · 3 phone brands · a page each`, with the source line and the context `No performance data yet.` The film ends on `Ask us again in three months.`
