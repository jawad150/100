Hulk Marketing: Static Post Concept

"Big Shadow" · 1080 × 1350 (4:5) · Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • The best-performing minimalist ads use one object and one clever visual twist, for example an everyday object casting a shadow of something much bigger, or a product turned into a symbol.
  • Lots of negative space, one short headline and a small logo. The image does the talking.
  • Bold flat background colours with a single strong shadow or light source.

Our angle: use the shadow trick to show exactly what Hulk does for a business: make something small look and feel big.

The Idea

A single plain business card stands on a desk. The light hits it, and its shadow on the wall behind is the shape of a giant city billboard. It's a small business with a big presence. In one glance, the viewer understands what good marketing does: it makes a small business impossible to miss.

Visual

  • One object only: a cream business card standing upright on a clean desk surface, with a tiny green "H" printed on it.
  • One strong light from the side throws a long, sharp shadow on the wall behind, shaped like a large billboard on a tall post.
  • Background wall in deep forest #111A16 with a soft light pool, so the shadow reads clearly. Desk surface slightly lighter.
  • Realistic photo or 3D render, sharp, calm and premium. Lots of empty space above the object for the headline.
  • No other props, icons or decoration.

AI / 3D Prompt

"Minimalist advertising photo, a single cream business card standing upright on a clean dark desk, strong side light casting a long sharp shadow on the dark green wall behind shaped like a giant city billboard on a tall pole, deep forest green background (#111A16), soft pool of light, lots of negative space at the top, premium, realistic, high detail, no text."

Tip: if the AI doesn't get the billboard shadow right, shoot or render the card on its own and add the billboard shadow as a separate layer in Photoshop.

Layout (1080 × 1350)

  1. Headline at the top: Inter Tight 900, about 96 px, cream #F4F2E9, two lines.
  2. The card and its billboard shadow in the centre and lower half: the focal point.
  3. Supporting line under the image: Hanken Grotesk 400, 34 px, cream at 80%.
  4. CTA button: green #9DEF69 pill, forest text, Hanken Grotesk 600, 34 px.
  5. Logo, small, bottom right.

Rules: two fonts only, minimum text size 34 px, 80 px margins, no more than about 20 words on the post.

Colours

  • Forest #111A16: background wall and shadow
  • Cream #F4F2E9: business card and text
  • Hulk green #9DEF69: the small "H" on the card and the CTA button only

Copy

Headline: Small business. Big presence.

Supporting line: We make sure the right people can't miss you.

CTA button: Get my free audit ↗

Alternative headlines:

  • Your business is bigger than people think.
  • Look bigger. Grow bigger.

Why It Works

  • One object, one idea, understood in under a second.
  • Clear hierarchy: headline, then image, then button.
  • Readable on mobile: large text, high contrast, lots of space.
  • Conversion-focused: one specific, free offer as the CTA.
