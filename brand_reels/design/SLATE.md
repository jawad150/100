## 2. What I changed from the judges' versions (and why)

1. **C26 counter arithmetic (a real error in the panel draft).** v1 plus 26 change requests = v27, and the rewind is
   Ctrl+Z ×26. Hook B said "27 REVISIONS BAAD" over a v27 counter, which is off by one. Locked: hook B text `*26* /
   REVISIONS BAAD`, VO "Chhabbees revision baad, client ne kaha...". The counter steps only when a pin lands.
2. **C02 BPM 150 (felt as 75) → 85.714.** 150 is the same grid as C15's 75 (bible §2.1), so the two reels would share a
   tempo. 85.714 BPM is frame-locked (21 f/beat), is the bible's "warm memory" tempo and sits in `lofi_desi`'s range.
   DUR 13 bars = 36.4 s.
3. **Typing dots belong to C02 only.** C26's suspense beat (23.47 s) becomes a last pin hovering over the frame,
   undecided, instead of typing dots.
4. **Faces re-allocated** so no crop and no hero expression appears in two reels (viral had the streetwear 3/4 turn in
   C26 and C15, and a smirk as hero in C26 and C08).
5. **Families:** C26 owns digital/editor (D9), so C08 takes camera and C15 takes organic (the director had C08 digital
   and C15 camera). The orbits in C08 and C15 are in-shot `K.Cam` moves, not transitions (`C4`/`C5` are spec-only in
   `jawad_tx`).
6. **End cards are ≥ 4.0 s.** `endcard.EndCard` enforces dur 4.0-6.0 s and a settled hold ≥ 1.5 s (settle 1.85 s with
   the JD monogram, exit 0.36 s). The viral maps' 2.1-3.2 s end cards would raise a ValueError. Each reel's end-card
   start is on its grid (§3).
7. **C15:** no floodlight clunk at the open (one clunk only, at the warm turn, 25.6 s); dim catch-light eyes make the
   head-snap readable at distance (production); hook B is `YEH *log* HAIN KAUN?`, so no crowd count has to be verified;
   the payoff keyword is *busy* (director), not the stock line "apni kahani khud likho"; no first-person "Main editor
   hoon" from an AI voice.
8. **C11:** the cheer is a text slam plus a wordless CC0 cheer (`crowd_cheer_real`, low) and the appliance chorus; no
   synthesised voices. A desktop PC (dies instantly), never a laptop "unsaved" dialog. The re-hook moves to 16.0 s
   (bar 6, on the grid).
9. **C08:** the spoken hook drops फ़्रेम ("Aap ne ise palak jhapakte dekha.", 6 words) so the pronunciation risk leaves the
   first 3 s; no large centred play/pause ring (his OpenArt cover device); the hidden JD sits in the bokeh layer from
   frame 0.
10. **C26:** the bonus pin under the end card is dropped (three text blocks); the cream "white background" gag is
   capped (inside the player only, player interior ≤ 35 % of the frame, IVORY ×0.8, v6-v12 only).
11. **CTA lines measured:** "FAMILY GROUP MEIN" is 937 px at 86 px (2 px inside the safe zone), so C02's card reads `GROUP
   MEIN / *bhejo*`. `26 REVISIONS BAAD` (913 px) is split into a keyword line and a caps line.
