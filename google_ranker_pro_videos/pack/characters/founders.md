### 7. Voice memos

- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings (a lounge or bedroom, not the kitchen). Hold the phone about 30 cm from the mouth, slightly off to one side.
- Use the Voice Memos app on its lossless setting, or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder. Mono, one voice, no music or TV. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. If a take clips, record it again.
- Make **one file per line**, with three takes inside it and a 2-second gap between takes. Each take is 5–8 s, said the way he would say it on the phone to a client, not read out.
