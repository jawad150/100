01 · The briefHulk Marketing · Static post concept

What we’re making

One post that proves the promise.

Hulk’s site says it plainly: “We turn ambitious businesses into brands people notice, choose and come back to.” The post should demonstrate the first word, notice, instead of claiming it.

  • Objective. Brand awareness and recall: saves, shares and profile visits from business owners who see it.
  • Audience. Founders and marketing leads at ambitious UK businesses who feel their brand looks like everyone else’s.
  • Single-minded message. Standing out is the job, and Hulk does it.
  • Tone. Confident, warm, a little cheeky. “Anything but ordinary,” never shouty.
  • Mandatories. Real logo, brand colours (forest, cream, green), the site’s fonts, CTA “Let’s make your next move ↗”, URL.
  • Platforms. Instagram and Facebook feed (4:5), LinkedIn (1:1 or 4:5), Stories (9:16).

03 · Insight → ideaHulk Marketing · Static post concept

The insight

People don’t remember what you claim. They remember what they notice.

Most businesses don’t have a visibility problem; they have a sameness problem. Same templates, same promises, same look as the rest of their category.

The idea: The Spot Test

Let the viewer prove it to themselves.

  • 1 · They see a grid. Rows of identical grey dots: every brand that blends in.
  • 2 · They find the green one instantly. No thinking required. That’s the point.
  • 3 · The payoff lands. “Found it? Of course you did. That’s what standing out looks like.”
  • 4 · Hulk owns the moment. The green dot is Hulk’s green: the same character as the brand reel, so post and reel read as one campaign.
Why it works: participation beats persuasion; one action, one message, one colour04
04 · The post · 4:5 feed (1080 × 1350)Hulk Marketing · Static post concept

Primary execution

Quiet in a loud feed.

Forest field, one block of type, forty-two grey dots and a single green one. In a feed of busy agency posts, restraint is the thing that stops the thumb.

  • First read (0–1 s): the green dot, then the headline.
  • Second read (1–3 s): “Found it? Of course you did.” The penny drops.
  • Third read: the line in Hulk’s own words, then the CTA and URL.

  • 1 · Logo + tag. The real HULK MARKETING wordmark (vector-traced from the site), 250 px wide. Tag “Anything but ordinary” in Hanken Grotesk 22 px, tracked 0.18em.
  • 2 · Headline. Inter Tight 900, 112 px, −0.045em. “remember.” in Inter Tight 300 italic: the site’s bold/italic pairing.
  • 3 · The grid. 9 × 5 dots, 60 px diameter on a 104 px pitch, colour #2C3B33: a dark step of the forest so they recede.
  • 4 · The green dot + callout. Hulk green #9DEF69, the only accent. A 3 px rule runs to “THAT ONE”; the grey dots under the rule are removed so it reads cleanly.
  • 5 · Payoff. Inter Tight 800, 54 px: “Found it? Of course you did.”
  • 6 · Proof line. Hanken Grotesk 28 px in muted sage #9AA398, the site’s own positioning line.
  • 7 · Action bar. Green pill “Let’s make your next move ↗” (Hanken 600, 28 px) and the URL, above a hairline rule.
Margins 72 px · every element left-aligned to one edge except the centred grid06

10 · Specs + publishing checklistHulk Marketing · Static post concept

Deliverables

  • Instagram / Facebook feed · 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) PNG, dark and light
  • LinkedIn / Facebook · 1080 × 1080 px (1:1) PNG
  • Stories · 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) PNG, safe zones respected
  • Colour · sRGB · export PNG (flat colour stays crisp; avoid JPEG banding on the forest)
  • Source · built in code (HTML/CSS); every size re-renders from one file

Before it goes live

  • Pair it with the reel · post the static a few days after the brand reel so the green dot is already familiar
  • Pin it to the top of the Instagram grid alongside the reel
  • Alt text + caption from the copy deck; reply to every “I’m a grey dot” comment
  • Measure saves, shares, profile visits and link clicks against your own recent posts in native analytics
  • Check the logo’s ® and the URL at phone size before posting
