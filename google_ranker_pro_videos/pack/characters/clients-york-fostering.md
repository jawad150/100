# Character sheets: York Cars and Organic Fostering (CLIENT_YORK_MANAGER, CLIENT_OF_DIRECTOR)

These are two real clients, each filmed in their own real workplace: the York Cars manager in the yard and dispatch office (V05), and the Organic Fostering director or registered manager at the office table in Beaumont House (V06). Neither business names anyone publicly, so **you ask each client who will appear and how they want to be captioned**. Both are **Mode B**. The face comes only from their own photos, used with written consent. The words are their own answers, transcribed by us and approved by them. The voice is their own voice memo. **No prompt ever describes their face.** Every shot puts the person inside a real place, so the location also comes only from the client's own photos: **no York Cars photos means no V05**, and no Beaumont House photos means V06 runs as the text-only fallback. Work through each person in order: consent, photos, sheet, crops, voice.

**Before you start, for both people**
- **Settings for every sheet:** Nano Banana Pro (`nano_banana_pro`) · 3:2 · 4k · count 4, pick the best of the four. On the Higgsfield MCP, load the `character-sheet` workflow first.
- **What a sheet prompt contains:** the B.2 layout, the wardrobe line copied exactly, one skin line and one imperfection. It contains **no description of the face, hair, skin tone, age or build**.
- **On-screen label (we add it in the edit):** `AI re-creation made with [Name]'s permission. Their words, their voice. Real data.` If the client prefers a role to a name, use the role, e.g. "the operations manager at York Cars".
- **Client words** stay as `[APPROVED LINE n FROM THEIR TRANSCRIPT]` until the client approves them in writing. They quote no numbers. All numbers appear on screen in the edit, with their source.

**How to record**
- A quiet room with soft furnishings, door shut, away from the main road. Phone about 30 cm from the mouth, slightly off to one side.
- Voice Memos on its lossless setting, or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder. Mono, one voice, no music or TV. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS; re-record any take that clips.
- Say it the way they would to a prospective carer on the phone: calm, unhurried, not read out.
- **What they may mention:** the plain-English guides and the postcode check. **What they must not say:** any number, allowance or income figure, '24/7', the Ofsted grade (unless the PDF has been checked), any traffic or enquiry result, or any carer's or child's name or story. Their accent is never described anywhere in text.
- If the generated voice does not sound like them, it fails QC. Fix it in the order in production_rules G.1.
