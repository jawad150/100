Hulk Marketing: Animated Video Concept

"The Marketing Gym" · 30 seconds · 2D character animation · 9:16 (with 1:1 and 16:9 cuts)

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • Business animation boards are full of the same flat-vector office people: suited figures shaking hands, men holding briefcases, silhouettes in front of skylines, rising bar charts.
  • Explainer storyboards almost all follow the same structure: hook, problem, solution, how it works, benefits, call to action.
  • The pieces that stand out give an everyday object a personality (a character-led promo) and use one tight colour palette instead of generic blues.

Our angle: keep the proven problem-to-solution structure, but drop the suited office people. The characters are the client's marketing itself, and Hulk's "marketing with muscle" idea becomes a literal gym.

Concept

Most businesses' marketing is out of shape. Their website is a couch potato, nobody can find them on the map, their ads fly in circles and the owner is buried in admin. In this video those problems are characters. Coach H (the Hulk "H" icon with a whistle) puts them through a training montage, and they come out fit, fast and all pulling in the same direction.

The four problems come straight from the "Start where it matters" section of hulkmarketing.co.uk:

  1. My website needs to work harder.
  2. The right people aren't finding us.
  3. Our advertising needs direction.
  4. Manual work is slowing us down.

Tagline: Marketing with muscle.

Characters

  • Coach H: the green Hulk "H" icon with small arms and legs, a whistle and a clipboard. Strict but cheerful. The brand mascot.
  • Web: a browser window with little legs and a sweatband. Starts slumped on a sofa with a slow loading spinner for a face.
  • Pin: a map pin. Starts lying flat and see-through, so nobody can find it.
  • Ad: a paper plane. Starts looping in circles and bumping into walls.
  • The Owner: a friendly small-business owner (the only human), buried under sticky notes at the start.
  • Bot: a small robot arm that arrives in the montage to take over the manual work.

Style

  • Flat 2D vector with chunky rounded shapes, thick outlines and squash-and-stretch movement. Playful, not corporate.
  • Colours: forest #111A16 and cream #F4F2E9 backgrounds, ink #20392E outlines, Hulk green #9DEF69 as the only accent. Coach H and all the "after" versions wear green.
  • Type: Inter Tight (headlines) and Hanken Grotesk (labels), the website's own fonts.
  • Problem scenes use cream and muted, droopy colours. From the whistle onwards the background switches to the forest-green gym and everything turns energetic.

Scene by Scene

Scene 1 · 0:00–0:03 · The hook

Visual: Cream background. Web is slumped on a sofa eating crisps, its loading spinner face turning slowly. It yawns.

On-screen text: Is your marketing out of shape?

Voice-over: "Is your marketing… a bit out of shape?"

Sound: A snore, a slow ticking spinner, crisp crunch.

Scene 2 · 0:03–0:09 · The problems (four quick gags, 1.5 seconds each)

a. Web: tries to stand up from the sofa and gives up. Text: "My website needs to work harder."

b. Pin: lies flat and fades half-transparent while people walk past without seeing it. Text: "The right people aren't finding us."

c. Ad: the paper plane loops in circles and bonks into a wall. Text: "Our advertising needs direction."

d. The Owner: sticky notes pile up until only a hand pokes out. Text: "Manual work is slowing us down."

Voice-over: "A website that won't work… customers who can't find you… ads going nowhere… and admin everywhere."

Sound: A sad trombone slide on each gag (short and light), paper rustle, a soft bonk.

Scene 3 · 0:09–0:11 · The whistle

Visual: Coach H bounces into frame and blows a whistle. The background snaps from cream to the forest-green gym. All four characters jump to attention.

On-screen text: Welcome to the Hulk Marketing gym.

Voice-over: "Right. Training starts now."

Sound: A loud whistle, then the music kicks in.

Scene 4 · 0:11–0:21 · The training montage (cut to the beat, about 2.5 seconds per station)

Station 1 · Web: bench-presses a barbell. Its spinner face snaps into a sharp, fast homepage; a "Book now" button lights up green. Label: Website Design & Development.

Station 2 · Pin: does squats and grows taller and greener with each rep until it stands proudly on top of the map. Label: SEO & Local Maps.

Station 3 · Ad: Coach H draws a straight chalk line; the paper plane sprints along it and hits a bullseye dead centre. Label: PPC & Meta Ads.

Station 4 · Bot: a robot arm sweeps the sticky notes into one neat stack; the Owner pops out and sips a coffee. Label: AI Automation.

Voice-over: "Websites that work harder. Search that gets you found. Ads with direction. And AI that does the heavy lifting."

Sound: Weight clank, rep counts ("one… two…"), a whoosh and a thunk on the bullseye, a cash-register ding on "Book now".

Scene 5 · 0:21–0:25 · All pulling in the same direction

Visual: All the characters, now fit and wearing green, grab one rope and pull together in a tug-of-war. As they pull, the rope drags a big green arrow up and to the right across the screen. The Owner smiles as a shop bell rings and customers stream in.

On-screen text: Strategy. Creative. Digital. AI. All pulling in the same direction.

Voice-over: "Strategy, creative, digital and AI, all pulling in the same direction."

Sound: Team grunt, rope creak, a shop door bell.

Scene 6 · 0:25–0:30 · End card

Visual: The team strikes a group flex pose. Coach H gives a thumbs up, then hops into place as the icon of the HULK MARKETING logo. Forest background.

On-screen text: Marketing with muscle. · Get your free audit ↗ · hulkmarketing.co.uk

Voice-over: "Hulk Marketing. Marketing with muscle."

Sound: A final whistle blast and one punchy music hit.

Hulk Marketing. Marketing with muscle."

Music and Sound

  • 0–9 seconds: lazy, sleepy lounge loop that sags with each gag.
  • From the whistle at 9 seconds: an upbeat workout track (around 125 bpm, punchy drums, brass stabs), with cuts landing on the beat.
  • Cartoon sound effects throughout: whistle, weights, whooshes, boings, rep counts. Keep them crisp and not too loud under the voice.

Production Notes

  • Design a character sheet first (Coach H, Web, Pin, Ad, the Owner, Bot) in the brand colours, then animate in After Effects, Rive or a 2D tool of your choice.
  • Keep Coach H clearly an "H" icon with arms. Do not draw a green-skinned giant or anything that resembles Marvel's Hulk.
  • Each problem gag must read in under 1.5 seconds: one action and one line of text.
  • Captions on screen throughout, as most people watch with the sound off.
  • Cutdowns: a 15-second version can drop Scene 5 and shorten the montage to two stations per beat.
