## What this video is

- **Concept.** Three short portraits from one wet October morning in the agency's real room. **Rules for the lines**
- **T1's task** must come straight from the Golden Line work log, in about eight words, so the whole of line 2 takes 5 s or less. No causal verbs ("made", "drove", "got them to"), no rankings, no numbers.

- **No** "No.1", "first", "now", "last quarter" or growth words in any line.
- **"I make the tea"** stays only if it is true.
- Each memo is **one line**. Lines must be said in **5–6 s** at a natural pace. If a line runs longer, shorten the wording, not the pace.

**How to record a memo (send this to each person)**
- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings, such as a lounge or bedroom (not the kitchen or a stairwell). No TV, radio, fan or music. In Pakistan, turn the ceiling fan off.
- Hold the phone about **30 cm** from your mouth, slightly to one side, so your breath doesn't pop.
- Use Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder), mono. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- **One line per memo.** Record three takes of the line, each as its own file: `MEMO_<CASTID>_v10_line<n>_t1.m4a`, `_t2`, `_t3`. We keep the best as `MEMO_<CASTID>_v10_line<n>.m4a`, trimmed to the line plus 0.3 s.
- Say it the way you'd say it to a colleague who asked, not as if reading.
- Send full-size files by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Not WhatsApp.**
- Nobody's accent is ever described in a prompt. The memo carries the voice.

**Which memo goes where.** The memo is the `@Audio` reference in Seedance (it guides timbre and pace), and we lay the real memo in as the final track wherever sync allows. **If the generated voice doesn't sound like the person, it fails QC** (production_rules G.1).
