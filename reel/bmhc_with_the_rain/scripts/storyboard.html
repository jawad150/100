How this works

What I gathered

  • About 970 candidate clips were reviewed from Mixkit and Coverr, both free for commercial use. Every clip was checked frame by frame for faces, modest dress and anything unsuitable. Mixkit clips under its “Restricted” licence were rejected, because it doesn’t allow company social media posts.
  • Pexels, Pixabay and Videvo block automated access, and Magnific (the premium stock and AI video service connected here) has only 2 credits left. So no premium or AI-generated clips are used yet; see page 5 for what they would add.
  • BMHC’s own photos come from bmhc.org.uk: the Gothic tower and front door, graded to night with rain and warm light in the doorway. Instagram blocks automated access, so nothing was taken from @thebmhc. Any photos or clips you download from it can be swapped in.
  • Rain: the rain falling over dry clips and photos is generated in code, so it has no licence issues and always matches.
Best upgrade: a 1-minute phone video from BMHC (4K, vertical, at dusk) of the front door opening and the lit entrance hall, empty. Shots 7 and 8 would then be real footage instead of a photo with added light.

Recording the voiceover

  • A calm, warm British narrator, human (no AI voice). Unhurried and kind, not dramatic.
  • Record each line separately with a second of silence between, in a quiet room or booth.
  • Send WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit (an iPhone voice memo is a fallback). No music or effects on it.
  • Say “Ash-Sharh” and “Imam” naturally; the URL appears on screen, so it isn’t read out.

Recording the recitation

  • BMHC’s imam (or a qualified reciter) recites 94:5–6 once, slowly, as a separate file. No AI recitation.
  • The shot is sized to his take. It is never trimmed or sped up.
  • Ask the imam to check the Arabic on screen and the reflection on “with”. The text file is supplied with the concept.

Background music

You asked for proper background music. For a mosque, the respectful choice is a vocal-only nasheed-style bed with no instruments: soft layered humming that swells on “with” and on the door, dips under the voice, and goes completely silent under the recitation and the translation. Choose one:

  • 1 · Licensed vocal-only nasheed (recommended): a short “vocals only” or a cappella track from a library BMHC is happy with, or a nasheed artist BMHC knows. I edit it to the cut.
  • 2 · AI-generated vocal bed: made to fit the timing exactly with Magnific’s music tools. That needs about 1,000 credits for 50 seconds, and the account currently has 2.
  • 3 · Voice and rain only: no music, just rain, the city and the voice. It is free and ready now, and it is the safest choice if BMHC prefers no music.

Either way, the edit has full sound design: rain on glass and stone, a distant bus, an umbrella, a spoon in a cup, a soft phone buzz and the door. It is mixed for Instagram at −14 LUFS.
