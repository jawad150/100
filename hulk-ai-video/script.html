Hulk Marketing: AI Video Concept

"The Grey Street" · 20 seconds · 9:16 (with a 1:1 cut for LinkedIn)

Concept

On a high street where every shop looks the same, one small business gets noticed. Everything is grey: the shopfronts, the signs, the people walking past looking at their phones. Then a single drop of Hulk green lands outside one café, and colour floods its front. People stop, look up and walk in. It is Hulk's promise played out as a story: brands people notice, choose and come back to.

Tagline: Anything but ordinary.

Look

  • Cinematic, realistic UK high street, overcast morning, shot on 35 mm.
  • Everything fully desaturated, so the street looks black-and-white.
  • Hulk green (#9DEF69) is the first colour to appear, then natural colour returns only to the café.

Shot by Shot

Shot 1 · 0:00–0:03 · The hook

Visual: Wide, slow push-in down a grey British high street. Identical grey shopfronts, identical grey signs, people walking at the same pace with their heads down at their phones. Flat and lifeless.

On-screen text: Every brand on this street looks the same.

Sound: Muffled city ambience and a low, flat drone.

Shot 2 · 0:03–0:06 · The problem

Visual: Inside a small grey café. The owner (a woman in her 30s, apron, hair tied back) wipes an empty counter. Every table is empty. She glances at the door; people walk straight past the window.

On-screen text: So nobody remembers any of them.

Sound: A clock ticking, a door that doesn't open.

Shot 3 · 0:06–0:08 · The spark

Visual: Macro slow motion. A single drop of vivid green liquid falls and hits the grey pavement outside her door, splashing in a perfect crown. It is the only colour in the frame.

On-screen text: None.

Sound: Silence, then a deep bass hit on impact.

Shot 4 · 0:08–0:11 · The transformation

Visual: The green ripples outward from the splash and climbs the café front. The sign, awning, window frames and warm interior lights flood into rich colour, with green accents. The rest of the street stays grey.

On-screen text (one word per beat): Strategy. Creative. Digital. AI.

Sound: A rising whoosh into the music drop.

Shot 5 · 0:11–0:13 · Noticed

Visual: On the street, passers-by stop mid-stride, lift their heads from their phones and turn towards the café. One person raises a phone to photograph the shopfront.

On-screen text: Noticed.

Sound: Footsteps stopping, a camera shutter.

Shot 6 · 0:13–0:16 · Chosen, and come back to

Visual: Inside, the café is now warm and busy, with a queue at the counter and the owner smiling as she serves. Quick cut: next morning, a regular pushes the door open and waves to her.

On-screen text: Chosen. → Come back to.

Sound: Café chatter, cups, the door bell.

Shot 7 · 0:16–0:20 · End card

Visual: A drone crane pulls up and back over the street. Every shop is grey except one glowing café with a green accent. The frame settles and the end card fades over the top.

On-screen text: HULK MARKETING logo · Anything but ordinary. · Let's make your next move ↗ · hulkmarketing.co.uk

Sound: The music resolves on one held chord.

Voice-over (optional)

Calm, confident British voice:

"Most brands blend in…
so nobody remembers them.
(beat)
We build the ones people notice… choose… and come back to.
Hulk Marketing. Anything but ordinary."

AI Generation Prompts

Use these in Veo, Kling, Runway or Higgsfield. Generate a reference image of the café owner first and use it in every shot, so she looks the same throughout.

  1. Cinematic wide shot of a British high street on an overcast morning, every shopfront identical and grey, plain grey signs, pedestrians walking in sync looking down at their phones, fully desaturated black-and-white world, slow dolly push-in, 35 mm film look, realistic, 9:16.
  2. Inside a small grey café, woman in her 30s in an apron wiping an empty counter, all tables empty, she glances hopefully at the door as people walk past outside, fully desaturated, soft window light, handheld, realistic.
  3. Extreme macro slow motion: a single drop of vivid neon-green liquid (#9DEF69) falls onto grey wet pavement outside a café door and splashes in a crown shape, the only colour in a monochrome frame, high-speed camera, shallow depth of field.
  4. Green colour ripples out from a splash on the pavement and climbs up a café shopfront, the sign, awning and windows transforming from grey to rich warm colour with green accents, the rest of the street stays grey, smooth magical transition, cinematic.
  5. Pedestrians on a grey street stop walking, look up from their phones and turn towards one colourful café, one person takes a photo of it, natural reactions, slow motion, cinematic.
  6. Warm busy café interior full of colour, a queue at the counter, the same woman smiling as she serves customers, golden light; then the next morning a customer opens the door and waves, realistic, cinematic.
  7. Aerial drone crane shot rising over a grey monochrome high street where one café glows in colour with green accents, slow upward pull-back, cinematic, 9:16.

Negative prompt for every shot: cartoon, text, logos, distorted faces, extra fingers, green-skinned giant, superhero, oversaturated. The "superhero" and "green-skinned giant" exclusions keep the AI from producing anything that looks like Marvel's Hulk.

Music and Edit

  • Music: A minimal, muted pulse for the first 8 seconds that blooms into a warm, confident groove when the green hits.
  • Edit: Add the on-screen text and the end card in your editor, not in the AI generation, so the logo and spelling stay exact.
