## What this video is

- **Concept.** A Warwick taxi owner, parked up before sunrise, tells us in his own words what it was like when Google sent no work his way, what changed when the new site went live, and what's different now. Problem (his own car, pre-dawn) → turn (his own back office at dusk, laptop open with a green screen) → result (the real Search Console receipts).
- **Hook.** Black. A wiper thunks once. A phone lights up face-up on a dark dashboard and buzzes against the plastic. Over it, the first sentence of his real answer. No title for 6 s. (9:16: the same image with `312 clicks · 18.2K times shown` on screen by 0.8 s.)
- **Why this brand.** Golden Line is the only client with a verified Hulk credit on its live site (footer: "Made with ❤ by Hulk Marketing Ltd") **and** an attributed Search Console screenshot.
- **What the edit reveals.** At ~37 s the live footer credit is pushed in as proof of the work. At 49 s (65% of the runtime) the real totals land on his laptop's green screen and push to full frame: 312 clicks, 18.2K times shown, 744 different searches, brand searches top two on average (1.8), two airport searches on page one on average. He says no numbers. The numbers are ours, on screen, sourced and dated.
- **The motif.** The film opens and closes on a phone lighting up, its screen never readable.
