BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A changing search landscape does not need to mean vague promises. Here is how we approach it.

Built on established guidance.

Read Google’s own advice on AI search features and helpful content.

What do you mean by AI SEO, AEO and GEO?

AI SEO is our name for SEO work focused on making your business information clearer and more accessible for search and AI-assisted discovery. AEO means answer engine optimisation; GEO means generative engine optimisation. We explain the actual work rather than sell these labels as a separate ranking shortcut.

Can you guarantee that AI tools will recommend my business?

No. We can improve your website’s information and technical foundations, but we cannot control an AI system’s answers or guarantee a citation, recommendation or ranking. The hero illustration demonstrates useful page content, not a live AI result.

Do I need special AI schema or an llms.txt file?

Google’s guidance says there is no special schema or new AI text file required for AI Overviews or AI Mode. Appropriate structured data should describe the content people can actually see. We prioritise genuine technical and content needs, rather than treating a file or markup as a guarantee.

Is this a replacement for my existing SEO?

No. The work builds on technical SEO, useful content and a coherent website structure. We review what is already being done, identify any overlap and agree a scope before recommending additional work. You do not automatically need another full retainer.

What will the visibility review cover?

We discuss your website, key services and customer questions, then assess the technical and content priorities relevant to the agreed review. You will know the scope, any access required and the proposed next steps before implementation is agreed. This website does not run an instant automated scan.

How will you measure progress?

Use agreed reporting periods, relevant search and enquiry data, and identifiable referral traffic where available. Google reports its AI-feature traffic within overall Web search performance in Search Console; it is not a clean standalone AI citation report. Any manual answer checks should state the prompts, dates and limitations.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

That depends on the website, the number of services, the current content and the implementation needed. Request a review so we can agree a scope and proposal. We do not advertise an unconfirmed AI SEO fee or promise visibility within a fixed number of days. Your existing SEO plans may already cover some of the work.
