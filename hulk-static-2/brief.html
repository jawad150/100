Hulk Marketing: Static Post Concept

"After Hours" · 1080 × 1350 (4:5) · Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn

The Idea

Every business owner knows the moment after closing: the shop is quiet, the lights are low, and they are still working on a laptop, wondering why more people don't find them. The post shows one real, honest moment like that and makes one promise: you built the business, we'll bring the people. It works through emotion instead of decoration.

Visual

  • One full-bleed photo of a real small-business owner after hours: a café owner on a stool behind the counter, laptop glow on their face, chairs stacked on the tables, warm lights. Tired but determined, not posed.
  • Documentary style: natural light, slight grain, nobody looking at the camera, nothing staged or "stock".
  • The only brand colour is the green CTA button. A soft forest-green gradient (#111A16) fades up from the bottom third so the text reads clearly over the photo.
  • No decoration: no hearts, notes, plants, icons or doodles. Just the photo, one message and one button.

Photo Brief

Use a real client (with consent) or a commissioned shoot, which is best for authenticity. If you need AI as a stand-in, use this prompt:

"Documentary photo, small independent café after closing time, owner in their 30s sitting behind the counter working on a laptop, chairs stacked on tables, warm low lamp light, quiet and tired but determined expression, candid and unposed, natural film grain, 35 mm, shallow depth of field, realistic, no text."

Layout (1080 × 1350)

Reading order, top to bottom:

  1. Photo filling the frame, with the owner's face in the upper half.
  2. Headline in the bottom third: Inter Tight 900, about 88 px, cream #F4F2E9, two lines maximum.
  3. One supporting line: Hanken Grotesk 400, 34 px, cream at 80% opacity.
  4. CTA button: green #9DEF69 pill with forest #111A16 text, Hanken Grotesk 600, 34 px.
  5. Logo, small in the bottom right (about 220 px wide).

Rules for the designer:

  • Two fonts only: Inter Tight and Hanken Grotesk. No handwritten type.
  • Minimum text size 34 px, so everything is readable on a phone.
  • 80 px margins on every side; 40 px between headline, line and button.
  • No more than about 20 words on the whole post.

Colours

  • Forest #111A16: gradient behind the text
  • Cream #F4F2E9: headline and supporting line
  • Hulk green #9DEF69: CTA button only

Copy

Headline: You built the business. We'll bring the customers.

Supporting line: SEO, ads and websites that work as hard as you do.

CTA button: Get my free audit ↗

Why this CTA: "Find out more today" asks for nothing and promises nothing. "Get my free audit" is a specific, free and personal next step, and it's an offer already on Hulk's website.
