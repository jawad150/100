# "Chandler Explains" — Margin Trading carousel: launch strategy

## 1. The idea in one line
A sitcom-style 3D carousel where the clever friend explains **margin trading** to the
clueless friend, using a PS5 and GTA 6, then reveals it is exactly how commodity
margin trading on **PMEX** works.

**Why it can spread:** nostalgia (a 90s sitcom the audience already loves), gaming (PS5 and GTA 6
are trending), and money (what most young Pakistanis are curious about). Each of the three
pulls in a different audience, so one post reaches three groups of people.

## 2. Audience
| Segment | Hook that lands |
|---|---|
| 18–30 Friends fans (IG / TikTok) | "Chandler explains. Joey... tries." |
| Gamers waiting for GTA 6 | "What if you could lock today's PS5 price?" |
| First-time investors in Pakistan | "Margin trading in 10 slides, no jargon" |
| Finance / LinkedIn crowd | "The simplest explanation of futures margin I've seen" |

## 3. Where to post (one asset, five formats)
1. **Instagram carousel (primary):** all 10 slides at 1080×1350. Post the 4:5 JPGs directly.
2. **LinkedIn document post:** export the 10 slides as one PDF. Document posts get much higher
   dwell time than single images on LinkedIn.
3. **TikTok Photo Mode:** same 10 images with a trending upbeat 90s-sitcom-style sound
   (**not** the official theme song, which is copyrighted).
4. **Reel / Short (15–20 s):** slides with a slow Ken-Burns zoom, 1.5 s each, text animated on
   the beat. The masking-text cover makes a strong first frame.
5. **Pinterest:** pin the cover and slide 8 ("Terms used in margin trading"). Pinterest favours
   evergreen "explainer" pins and keeps sending traffic for months.
6. **Facebook page and groups:** carousel plus a question in the caption (see section 5).

## 5. Engagement mechanics
- **Comment-keyword funnel:** "Comment **MARGIN** and I'll DM you the PMEX beginner checklist."
  Comments boost reach, and the DM gives you a warm lead.
- **Question in caption:** "Would YOU take Chandler's deal? Yes / No 👇", which gets easy replies.
- **Stories (same day):** poll on slide 3 "Will GTA 6 release on time?", then reveal slides 4/5
  as the answer. Quiz sticker on slide 8 terms.
- **Pin a comment** with the one-line definition from slide 10.

## 6. Timing (Pakistan, PKT)
- Post **Tue–Thu, 8:00–10:00 pm PKT**, when evening scrolling peaks. Second option: Sunday
  12:00–2:00 pm.
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes; early engagement drives distribution.
- Reshare to Stories at +3 h and +24 h.
- Treat these as starting hypotheses. Check Instagram Insights after 3 posts and shift.

## 7. Turn it into a series ("Chandler Explains", 1 episode a week)
| Ep | Topic | Story hook |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Leverage | Joey thinks Rs. 10,000 controls "a whole PlayStation store" |
| 3 | Margin call | The broker calls Joey mid-sandwich |
| 4 | Stop-loss | Chandler sets an alarm before the price falls too far |
| 5 | Long vs short | Joey bets the PS5 price will **drop** |
| 6 | Expiry & rollover | The 30 days are up, now what? |

Same characters, same finishing pipeline (`finish.py`), same fonts. A recognisable format makes
people follow the account to see the next episode.

## 8. Amplification
- **Collabs:** co-post (Instagram "Collab" feature) with 2–3 Pakistani personal-finance creators.
- **Communities:** share in finance / investing groups only where the rules allow
  promotional posts; lead with the educational value.
- **Paid boost (optional):** small test (e.g. PKR 3–5k over 3 days) on the best-performing
  organic post only, targeting 18–34, interests: investing, stock market, gaming.
  ⚠️ Meta's financial-services ad policy and SECP / PMEX rules on broker promotion apply:
  keep the risk disclaimer and avoid profit promises.

## 9. What to measure
| Metric | Why | Good target |
|---|---|---|
| Saves ÷ reach | Carousel quality signal | > 2 % |
| Shares ÷ reach | Virality ("send to your Joey") | > 1 % |
| Slides viewed / swipe-through | Story holds attention | Most viewers reach slide 5+ |
| Comments with keyword | Lead generation | Track weekly |
| Follows from post | Series growth | Track per episode |

A/B idea: next episode, test two covers (masking-text cover vs. close-up of Joey's confused face).

## 10. Risks to manage
- **Likeness / IP:** the characters resemble real actors and a famous show. Keep it clearly
  educational and parody, add the "fan-made, not affiliated" line, don't use the show's logo or
  theme song, and avoid using these images in **paid** ads or as an endorsement of a specific broker.
- **Financial promotion:** always keep the risk line; never show guaranteed profits.
- **Accuracy:** the numbers in slides 2–5 are consistent (Rs. 200,000 → ±Rs. 20,000 on
  Rs. 10,000 margin). Keep that consistency in future episodes.
