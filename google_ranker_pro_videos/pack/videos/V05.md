## What this video is

- **Concept.** On a wet Saturday night the York Cars manager walks us from the yard, through the office door, into the dispatch room, where York's night runs through one room. It is built only from York Cars' own photos of their yard and office. **Without those photos there is no V05.**
- **Hook.** A car door thunks in the wet yard on frame 1. The manager is already striding away from it, glances back at the phone filming them, and starts talking at second 1.
- **Why this brand.** York Cars is a 24/7 private-hire firm whose website is built for local search: a page for each of 15 suburbs and villages and 13 airports, and 2,695 Google reviews shown on the site. A night-shift tour shows the real business behind those pages.
- **What the edit reveals.** At 20 s (67%) the real review count from their own site lands as a receipt over the dispatcher's hands. At 24 s, over real York night footage, a capture of the site's navigation shows the location and airport pages. Both carry the caption `Context, not an agency result.` This is context about the client. It is **not** a result we claim.

**How to record a memo (send this to them):**
- One approved line per memo: three takes in the same file with a 2-second gap. Each take 5–6 s.
- Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings (a lounge or bedroom). Not the dispatch room with the radios on, and not the yard.
- Hold the phone about **30 cm** from the mouth, slightly to one side so the breath doesn't pop.
- Use Voice Memos on its lossless setting, or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder, in mono. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. Re-record any take that distorts.
- Say it the way you'd tell a new driver on their first night, not as if reading it out.
- Send full-size files by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Not WhatsApp.**
- Their accent is never described anywhere in a prompt. The memo carries the voice.

**Which memo goes where:** the best take is `@Audio 1` in W-01 (line 1) and W-02 (line 2). In the edit we lay their real memo in as the final voice wherever sync allows, with a light phone-at-1.5-metres EQ and the yard or room ambience under it. **If the generated voice doesn't sound like them, it fails QC** (production_rules G.1).
