## Fact check (SRA)
- **SQE1:** two Functioning Legal Knowledge exams (FLK1, FLK2). Each has 170 single-best-answer questions, split into two sessions of 85, sat on separate days. Ethics is tested throughout.
- **SQE2:** 16 stations (4 oral, 12 written), covering client interview and attendance note, advocacy, case and matter analysis, legal research, legal writing and legal drafting.
- **Order:** SQE1 must be passed (or exempted) before SQE2.
- **Other requirements to qualify:** a degree or equivalent, two years' qualifying work experience, and the SRA's character and suitability requirements.
- **IC London's SQE course page:** describes the course as delivered online, at your own pace. The script doesn't claim specific features beyond that.
