**Common look of all five:** waist-up to chest-up framing, centred, eye-level, direct to camera, 9:16, shallow depth of field. Soft, even, high-key daylight from a window to camera side, with almost no shadow contrast. A neutral palette of navy, white and pale blue (it matches the brand blue), each with one warm orange or amber prop that echoes the brand orange. They read as polished stock-corporate. Against the user's realism rule they are **too clean**: smooth, even skin, uniformly perfect teeth, catalogue wardrobe with no creases, symmetrical tidy rooms, over-even lighting, and exaggerated mouth shapes (#5) that read as AI lip-sync. New characters should **differ deliberately**:
- Use real-room clutter and practical light: mixed colour temperature, a window blowing out slightly, a visible lamp.
- Show real skin texture (pores, under-eye, slight shine) and lived-in clothing.
- Use handheld or phone-on-tripod micro-movement and imperfect framing.
- Use a British-regional casting mix that suits Midlands taxi and SME clients.
- Wardrobe could keep a navy or pale-blue thread for brand continuity.
