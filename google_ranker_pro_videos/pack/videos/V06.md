## What this video is

- **Concept.** One plain question on screen, "What was it actually like working with Hulk?", answered by Organic Fostering's director or registered manager at the table in their own office at Beaumont House on Chester Road. A mug of tea they actually drink. A bus passes soft in the wet window behind them. Adults only.
- **Hook.** A plain question card (never a fake comment bubble) over real footage of Chester Road in drizzle with a bus passing (RF06, never a tram).

## Script and voice memos

**DRAFT FOR CLIENT APPROVAL.** We send one question, never a sentence:

> "What was it actually like working with Hulk?"

**Step 1: the interview memo.** The director answers in their own words, as long as they like, in one file: `MEMO_CLIENT_OF_DIRECTOR_v06_interview.m4a`. We transcribe it and pick three lines. **They approve all three in writing** before recording them.

**What they may mention:** the plain-English guides, the postcode check, what the work was like day to day. **What they must not say:** any number, any allowance or income figure, "24/7", the Ofsted grade (unless the PDF has been checked), any traffic, ranking or enquiry result, any carer's or child's name or story, "No.1" or "first".

**Step 2: record the approved lines**, one file per line, three takes in each file with a 2-second gap.

If a chosen line runs longer than its slot, we pick a shorter one from the transcript; we never speed it up.

**Version B instead:** they approve a **two-sentence written statement** (no recording needed). We show it as text: `[APPROVED STATEMENT SENTENCE 1]` / `[APPROVED STATEMENT SENTENCE 2]`.

**How to record a memo (send this to the director):**
- One approved line per file, three takes in the same file, 2 seconds of silence between takes.
- A quiet room with soft furnishings, door shut, **away from the main road** (not the office by the window).
- Phone about **30 cm** from the mouth, slightly to one side so breath doesn't pop.
- Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1/48 kHz recorder), mono, one voice, no TV, radio or music. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- Say it the way you'd say it to someone thinking of fostering on the phone: calm, unhurried, not read out.
- Send full size by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Never WhatsApp.**
- Their accent is never described anywhere in a prompt. The memo carries the voice.

**If the generated voice in A-01 or A-02 doesn't sound like them, it fails QC.** We lay their real memo in as the final track wherever sync allows (production_rules G.1). Never ship a voice they haven't heard and approved.
