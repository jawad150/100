Hulk Marketing: Animated Video Concept

"Hulk OS" · 30 seconds · SaaS-style motion graphics · 9:16 (with a 16:9 cut for LinkedIn and the website)

The Idea

We launch Hulk Marketing the way a tech company launches software. The video looks and feels like a slick SaaS product launch: floating dashboard cards, a cursor clicking through features, smooth UI transitions. The "product" is Hulk OS, the operating system for your growth, with four modules: Strategy, Creative, Digital and AI.

Then comes the twist at the end: "Hulk OS isn't software. It's a team." The cards flip round to reveal the people behind each module. Business owners get the clarity and polish of a software product, with real people doing the work for them.

The Hook (first 2 seconds)

On-screen text: Your marketing has too many tabs open.

Visual: a browser crammed with chaotic tabs (SEO tool, ads manager, website builder, spreadsheet, social scheduler) and notification pop-ups stacking up and shaking. Every business owner recognises this feeling instantly.

Alternative hooks to test:

  • "What if your marketing worked like software?"
  • "Five tools. Three freelancers. Zero growth?"

Who It's For

  • UK business owners and marketing managers juggling several tools, freelancers and platforms without a joined-up plan.
  • Growing businesses that like modern, tech-led brands and want one partner instead of five suppliers.

Style

  • Clean SaaS motion graphics: floating rounded UI cards with soft shadows, slight 3D tilt and parallax, a cursor that clicks with a ripple, smooth eased transitions, kinetic typography.
  • Colours: forest #111A16 background, cream #F4F2E9 UI cards, ink #20392E text, Hulk green #9DEF69 for buttons, highlights and active states.
  • Fonts: Inter Tight (headlines) and Hanken Grotesk (UI labels), the website's own fonts.
  • The UI is a stylised mock-up, not a real product screen: simple shapes, short labels, no fake numbers or statistics.
  • Pace: a new UI moment every 1–2 seconds, cuts on the beat.

Scene by Scene

Scene 1 · 0:00–0:03 · Too many tabs (hook)

Visual: A browser window fills the screen. Tabs multiply across the top: SEO tool, Ads manager, Website builder, Spreadsheet, Social scheduler, Inbox. Notification pop-ups stack up and the whole window starts to shake.

On-screen text: Your marketing has too many tabs open.

Sound: A rapid build of notification pings.

Scene 2 · 0:03–0:05 · Close all

Visual: The cursor glides to the corner and clicks "Close all". Every tab is sucked into the centre and collapses into one app icon: the green Hulk "H".

On-screen text: Introducing Hulk OS.

Sound: A satisfying whoosh, then silence and a single soft chime.

Scene 3 · 0:05–0:08 · The dashboard opens

Visual: The icon expands into a clean dashboard. A sidebar slides in with four modules: Strategy, Creative, Digital, AI. Cards float into place one by one.

On-screen text: One system. Every channel.

Sound: The music starts: a minimal, confident electronic beat.

Scene 4 · 0:08–0:11 · Module 1: Strategy

Visual: The cursor clicks "Strategy". A roadmap card draws itself as a timeline; milestones pop in and tick green.

On-screen text: Strategy: a plan built around your goals.

Sound: Click, then three ticks.

Scene 5 · 0:11–0:14 · Module 2: Creative

Visual: The cursor clicks "Creative". A brand board assembles: colour swatches drop in, type samples slide across, and social post cards flip into a neat grid.

On-screen text: Creative people actually notice.

Sound: Card flips in rhythm.

Scene 6 · 0:14–0:17 · Module 3: Digital

Visual: The cursor clicks "Digital". A website wireframe snaps into a polished page; a search results card slides "Your business" to the top; an ad card toggles from "Off" to "Live" in green.

On-screen text: Website, SEO, ads and social, all connected.

Sound: Snap, slide, toggle.

Scene 7 · 0:17–0:20 · Module 4: AI

Visual: The cursor clicks "AI". An automation flow builds node by node (New lead → Instant reply → Added to CRM → Follow-up sent) while a green pulse travels along the line.

On-screen text: AI automation that does the busywork.

Sound: A soft electronic pulse moving from node to node.

Scene 8 · 0:20–0:24 · Built for leads

Visual: The four modules link up with connecting lines. On the right, notification cards stack up: "New enquiry", "New booking", "New 5-star review". A simple growth line rises across the background (no numbers on it).

On-screen text: All pulling in the same direction.

Sound: Three notification chimes rising in pitch.

Scene 9 · 0:24–0:27 · The twist

Visual: The dashboard pulls back. Each module card flips over to reveal a friendly flat-illustrated team member: a strategist, a designer, a developer, an AI specialist. They wave.

On-screen text: Hulk OS isn't software. It's a team.

Sound: Card flips, then a warm music lift.

Scene 10 · 0:27–0:30 · Call to action

Visual: Everything clears to the forest background. A green button appears: "Start your free audit ↗". The cursor clicks it and a ripple spreads, and the HULK MARKETING logo and URL settle underneath.

On-screen text: Start your free audit ↗ · hulkmarketing.co.uk

Sound: Click, ripple, final music hit.

Music and Sound

  • 0–3 seconds: a chaotic build of notification pings and a rising hum.
  • From "Close all": a sudden quiet, then a clean, minimal electronic beat (around 120 bpm) like a tech launch film.
  • UI sound design throughout: soft clicks, whooshes, ticks, toggles and chimes synced to every animation.
  • A warm lift on the twist, and one final hit on the CTA click.

Production Notes

  • Build in After Effects (or code-based motion graphics) using the brand colours and fonts; keep every UI card simple, with short labels only.
  • Do not show real third-party logos in the tabs (Google, Meta and so on); use generic labels such as "Ads manager" or "SEO tool".
  • No invented statistics or results on screen; the notification cards stay as general examples.
  • Captions on screen throughout for sound-off viewing.
  • Cutdown (15 seconds): Scenes 1, 2, 3, 8, 9 and 10.
