## 0. The idea in one paragraph

Every video looks like something a real person filmed on their own phone: a taxi owner propped on the dashboard between jobs, a founder talking to a phone on a desk tripod, a team member filming a laptop. Nothing looks produced, so nothing looks generated. The **phone footage is the human half** and the **real screen is the proof half**. Every claim lands on a real Search Console or Business Profile capture, a live-site recording or a footer credit. The proof is cut in the way creators really do it: as a duet, a stitch, a green-screen effect, or a phone filming a laptop. The formats are the ones people already scroll past every day. That familiarity makes the footage believable, and the real data makes it honest.

**Why this beats polish here**
- The user wants "real life, not AI/CGI/plastic". Phone footage has the most forgiving and most familiar texture there is: deep focus, small-sensor noise, auto exposure, handheld wobble, ungraded colour. Seedance gets it right more often than "premium" looks, which tend towards the glossy render the user hates.
- The agency's existing five presenter videos (`assets/sites/googlerankerpro/service_videos_contact.jpg`) are clean, even, stock-like and centred. This set is the opposite on purpose: mixed light, lived-in rooms, off-centre framing, real UK weather.
- googlerankerpro.com already embeds **1080×1920 portrait** service videos, so 9:16 masters work on the website as well as on Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
- Taxi-firm content on social is generic "call us" promos (creative_ideas §0), so the lane for real-feeling local footage is open.

## 1. Series grammar (applies to all 15)

### 1.1 Three rules for the first second
1. **Frame 1 is occupied and moving.** That could be a phone buzzing in a dash mount, a thumb mid-scroll, or a face already mid-breath. Generations keep their 1 s ambience handle (production_rules G.7). The edit **trims the handle off the social cut**, or lays the hook card over it, so the viewer never sees dead air.
2. **The number or the keyword is on screen by 0.8 s** (edit only), because TikTok and Shorts search results autoplay the opening (creative_ideas, Nonsensical source).
3. **There is a sound cause on frame 1:** a phone vibration on plastic, a car door, a laptop lid, a kettle click. All of it is native Seedance foley, never music.

### 1.2 The format kit (trend-aware, brand-safe)
| Format | What it looks like | Used in | Brand-safety rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard confessional** | Phone on a windscreen or dash mount, driver talking between jobs | 1, 4 | Parked, engine off, handbrake on. Never talking while driving |
| **Duet** | Top: a real screen recording. Bottom: front-camera reaction | 2 | The top half is always real data, never generated |
| **Stitch** | Opens on 1.5 s of an earlier video in the set, then a reply | 15 (stitches 1), 9 (stitches 3) | Stitch only our own approved clips |
| **Green-screen effect** | A person segmented over a real GSC page, as the app effect does | 14 | No physical green backdrop: we segment the person from their real room in the edit |
| **Phone filming a laptop** | Over-the-shoulder phone clip of a laptop with a flat green screen; the real capture is keyed in with glare and slight moiré | 8, 11 | The keyed content is a real capture with date and source visible |
| **Inverted focus** | Soft-focus foreground (a phone on the table), sharp person behind | 9 | — |
| **Then vs now (same framing)** | Two clips from the same spot, matched-cut, with the real chart revealed month by month | 4 | Both halves are labelled "dramatised"; the chart is the proof |
| **Selfie walk-and-talk** | Arm's-length front camera, walking, jump cuts per point | 3, 7, 12 | — |
| **Selfie tour** | Front camera walked through a real workplace | 5 | Real premises photos only |
| **Chair spin** | Person spins round in an office chair into frame and says their job | 10 | Real consenting staff only |
| **"Question we get asked"** | A plain question card, then the answer to a propped phone | 6 | **Never a fake "reply to @user" comment bubble.** Use a real comment only if it exists and its author agrees |
| **Day-in-the-life vlog** | Timestamped 3 to 5 s clips with one to-camera beat | 12 | The location must be real (UK or Pakistan, from the user) |

**Never:** trending copyrighted audio, meme templates that mock a client or a competitor, "No.1" captions, growth percentages, fake comment bubbles, fake notifications that show invented numbers, or Google logos or brand colours.

### 1.3 The proof pill (edit device, never generated)
- **Query pill:** a rounded pill with a small magnifier and the real query and value, e.g. `airport taxi warwick · avg position 5.3`.
- **Source line** under every number, in small grey text: `Google Search Console · 19 Jul–18 Oct 2026`, or `Google Business Profile · May–Oct 2026 (Oct part-month)`.
- **Number cards** show one big number with no ▲ arrows, because there is no comparison period.
- **"Average position"** is always written as "avg position" or "averaging". Position 1.8 is captioned "top 2", never "No.1".
- Type is Inter (the GRP site font). On social the accents are GRP orange `#F7931E` and blue `#3FA9F5` on `#0B1628`. If the user picks the Hulk brand for social, swap to lime `#C6FA61` on `#121C17`. Text stays inside the 9:16 safe zone (clear of the top 12% and bottom 20%).

### 1.4 Disclosure (persistent, small, lower-left above the safe zone)
- **Mode B real person:** "Recreated with AI from [Name]'s own photos and voice, with permission. Their words."
- **Mode A labelled presenter:** "AI presenter. Real client data." This matches the site's existing "AI-generated presenters and voices. Service explanations, not client testimonials."
- **Generic b-roll:** "Dramatised b-roll."
- Platform AI labels are switched on for every post (TikTok AIGC, YouTube altered/synthetic, Meta AI info). The exact wording needs the user's and their adviser's sign-off (production_rules §9).

### 1.5 Words in people's mouths
- **Clients:** we supply **questions and a beat structure**, not their answers. Any "draft line" below is marked DRAFT. The client rewrites it in their own words, records it as a voice memo (production_rules A.3) and approves the final cut. Numbers are spoken as words.
- **Founder and team:** we draft lines. They edit, approve and record them.
- **VO-only pieces (8, and the Version B of several others):** the voice is a **real recording** of the real person, laid in as audio. It is not generated. This is the most realistic voice there is, and it costs nothing.

### 1.6 Brand on screen
- **Social (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) and the UK client-facing pieces:** "Hulk Marketing", as the user's plan says.
- **googlerankerpro.com placements:** "Google Ranker Pro".
- **End card:** a lockup "Hulk Marketing · part of Google Ranker Pro", pending the user's choice (agency research §11.1).
- **Coventry caution:** coventryairporttaxis247.co.uk credits **SMEK** in its footer, and its review widget shows Global Cars 247 Slough reviews. Fix both on the site before any Coventry video names Hulk or shows the homepage on camera.

## 4. World Bible: "One wet October in the Midlands, filmed on phones"

**Time and weather:** mid to late October 2026. Overcast, drizzle on and off, wet tarmac, early dark (about 18:00). Leaves in gutters. Breath just visible outdoors in the early morning. This matches the GSC window (to 18 Oct) and today's date. v04's "May" half is the one exception: overcast spring green.

**Palette (things in front of the lens, not grading):**
- wet asphalt grey, kerb-stone concrete, UPVC white, red-brick terrace;
- private-hire silver, black and white bodywork;
- navy, charcoal and oatmeal clothing;
- one warm orange object per scene (a mug, a hi-vis strap seen at the edge, an indicator blink, a Post-it), which quietly echoes GRP orange `#F7931E`;
- sodium-orange and cool-white LED street light at night;
- dashboard instruments as small out-of-focus glows.

Brand colours appear **only** in edit graphics.

**Light logic (one named source per scene, verbatim across that scene's shots):**
- **In the car, daytime:** `Flat overcast daylight through the windscreen is the only key, soft and slightly cool; the side window camera-right adds a brighter edge on that cheek; the cabin roof and footwell fall into soft shadow; the windscreen itself is slightly overexposed.`
- **In the car, night:** `Cool-white LED street light from above and behind the car is the key, falling through the windscreen at a steep angle; the dashboard adds a faint warm under-glow; a faint rim from the street light separates the head from the dark cabin.`
- **Kitchen, home, office:** production_rules D.5 "UK office daylight" or "Mixed colour (evening office or home)" lines.
- **Outdoor:** production_rules D.5 "Outdoor UK overcast". **No golden hour in this set.** It is the most AI-looking light there is.
- **Deep skin tones:** add the D.5 exposure line.

**Texture lines (paste verbatim, one per video, never mixed inside a generation):** PHONE-SELFIE, PHONE-REAR and DOC-CAM, exactly as in production_rules D.1.

| Mode | Videos |
|---|---|
| PHONE-SELFIE (including "propped" camera lines) | 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12 (to-camera), 14, 15 |
| PHONE-REAR | 4, 5's b-roll, 8, 11, 12 (b-roll) |
| DOC-CAM | **13 only** (the homepage piece) |

**Propped-phone camera lines** (a PHONE-SELFIE variant for 1, 2, 6, 9, 14, 15):
- `The phone is propped in a mount or against an object at chest height, 50 to 70 cm from the face; the frame is static except for faint vibration when the surface is touched or a car door closes.`
- `Framing is a little off-centre, slightly low, the top of the head close to the top edge.`
- `Their eyes go to the screen image of themselves as often as to the lens.`

**Dressing anchors (every set gets two or three, plus one uncomposed element):**
- **Car:** a charging cable looped over the gearstick, a parking ticket face-down on the dash, a travel mug, a tree-shaped air freshener hanging plain with no text, a phone mount with a scuffed arm.
- **Office:** a half-drunk tea, a coat on the chair, a blank Post-it on the bezel, a chipped radiator, cables under the desk.
- **Kitchen:** a kettle, a fruit bowl, school-run clutter for adults only (keys, a lunch bag), condensation on the window.
- **Uncomposed element:** a passer-by half-cropped at the edge, a wiper parked mid-glass, a delivery van crossing the background.

**UK private-hire truth (taxi videos):**
- Private-hire cars are **not** hackney cabs. **No roof sign, no "TAXI" light, never queued on a station or hackney rank.** They pull up at kerbs, driveways, hotel forecourts and airport drop-off zones.
- Cars are generic, unbranded, and filmed three-quarter rear or with the plates hidden: a hybrid saloon (Prius or Corolla type), a Skoda Octavia or Superb type, a black people carrier (Galaxy or Sharan type), a Vito-type 8-seater.
- A real livery appears **only** from the client's own car photos (production_rules F).

**Forbidden list (whole set):**
- Readable text of any kind: signs, plates, mugs, papers, screens, clothing.
- Logos. Google marks or Google colours.
- Invented liveries, roof signs or council badges.
- Generated screen content: screens are angled away, defocused, or flat matte green with corners visible.
- Background music in generation; subtitles.
- Gimbal, drone, slider or dolly moves; golden hour; studio backdrops; "beauty" light; ring-light catchlights.
- Glass-walled corporate offices like the existing service videos, or a Big Ben or London skyline.
- Children's faces (Organic Fostering: never), real case details.
- Hype or polish tokens (D.6 strip list), camera or lens brands, the word "cinematic".
- A generated face for any real, named client. Any claim of "No.1", "x% growth", "first page one" (we do not know it was the first) or "just hit" (we cannot show recency).
- Talking on the phone or to camera while driving.

## 9. Edit and finish notes (us)

- **Pacing:**
  - Social pieces: a cut or 105–110% punch-in every 1.5–2.5 s; the payoff at 60–70% of the runtime; one call to action.
  - Web pieces (1, 2, 13, 15 web): 3–5 s holds on faces.

- **Sound:**
  - The native Seedance room tone and foley carry realism; never strip them.
  - Licensed music only, under −20 LU behind speech, or none (1, 6, 13 work without music).
  - No trending copyrighted sounds.
  - Masters at −14 LUFS.

  - Corner-pin the green on the four corners.
