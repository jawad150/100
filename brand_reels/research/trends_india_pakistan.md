## 1. Summary

1. **Reach mechanics haven't changed.** Watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach lead ranking. Sends matter
   most for reaching non-followers. The 30 Apr 2026 originality rules mean clean, wholly original exports with no
   reposted clips and no watermarks. Trial Reels let us test hooks on non-followers.
2. **What works in the region:** relatable "X vs Y" skits, one-performer multi-character sketches, mini-documentary
   storytelling, numbered lists, process→payoff reveals, Hinglish save-worthy explainers, emotional cinematic montages, and
   festival or hyper-local jokes. Caption-hook data shows **numbered lists beat the baseline on Reels (7.4 % vs 4.9 %)**,
   while "POV…" (4.4 %) and "This tool…" (2.4 %) underperform.
3. **Songs charting in BOTH countries (Spotify, week of 1 Oct 2026):** "Barsaat" (Banjaare), "Arz Kiya Hai" (Anuv Jain,
   Coke Studio Bharat), "Khat" (Navjot Ahuja), "Alfaaz" (Hamza Malik & Zain Zohaib), "Ashke" (Karan Aujla),
   "Ishq de Fanniyar (Female)", "Jo Tum Mere Ho". These are the safest picks for a cross-border reel. Anything tied to
   *Dhurandhar* is India-only and politically loaded.
4. **Calendar:** Navratri 11-19 Oct → Dussehra 20 Oct → Karwa Chauth 29 Oct → Diwali week 6-10 Nov (Ramayana Part 1
   reportedly releases into it) → Iqbal Day 9 Nov (PK) → Chhath 13-16 Nov → wedding season in both countries
   (India muhurats 21-26 Nov and 1-13 Dec, Pakistan "Decemberistan") → Black/"White" Friday 27 Nov → SRK's *King*
   24 Dec → Quaid-e-Azam Day / Christmas 25 Dec → year-end recaps.
5. **Tone guardrails:** India-Pakistan relations are tense (border incident 2 Oct 2026, Pakistani celebrity accounts
   blocked in India since 2025, India beat Pakistan in the Asian Games final on 3 Oct). Cross-border reels must stay on
   **shared** culture (poetry, weddings, chai, family, freelancing, cricket *as a feeling*, never as rivalry) and
   **shared** vocabulary.

## 10. Recommendations (exactly 3)

All three respect the plugin's hard rules: safe zones (key copy x 70-1010, y 230-1480; nothing in the bottom ~670 px or
the right ~230 px rail), verified copy only (no invented clients, numbers or awards), no full-frame flashes, a minimum
reading time (≥ 0.25 s per word, keyword lines ≥ 1.0 s settled), and burned-in captions inside the safe zone. They are
built only with Jawad's `jw_*` looks and new props, **never** the fostering/Floret looks, props or copy.

### R1: "Rewind reveal" process → payoff with numbered beats
- **What:** open on the finished cinematic frame (0-1.2 s, the payoff as a flash-forward), hard rewind (VHS-style
  scrub on a dark-glass timeline), then **3 numbered beats** ("1 · story, 2 · rhythm, 3 · light") that rebuild the
  shot, then the payoff again, now earned and looping back to frame 1.
- **Why:** "The process" is rising (F1). Process vs results and "post you working" (F5). Numbered lists are the best
  caption hook on Reels (A7: 7.4 % vs 4.9 %). Proof-over-polish (A13). The loop supports watch time and replays (A1).
- **Where:** hook = flash-forward + rewind (0-2.5 s); middle = numbered beats (2.5-28 s, one re-hook per beat); end
  = payoff + loop seam + `@jawad_mp4` card.
- **Build (toolkit):** `ui.app_window(header=..., title=...)` as the NLE (custom header and title, never the defaults),
  `ui.progress_ring` / scrub bar; `T.Glyphs('1', 'jw_key', ...).slam` for numbers; `J.HouseTitle('STEP', 'rhythm')`
  for each beat title + `J.underline`; rewind = `K.whip_blur` + `K.zoom_blur` with `samples()` 5-7 on the scrub; the
  payoff world on `K.background('jw_*')` + `J.embers()`; cues `whip`, `glitch_short` (rewind), `ui_click`, `riser` →
  `impact_big` on the payoff; `render.py --sheet 16` for timing checks. VO + SFX only, BPM grid via `K.beat`.
- **Risk:** reads as a tutorial if it's slow (keep each beat ≤ 7 s, show results, don't lecture). Uses UI that
  resembles real NLEs, so no Adobe/Apple logos. If the "finished frame" isn't strong, the hook dies, so pick the best
  render first.
- **Re-check after:** 2027-01-08.

### R2: "Arz kiya hai…" kinetic shayari about editor life (original couplets, VO-led, song-ready)
- **What:** a Hinglish/Roman-Urdu VO opens with the mushaira line "Arz kiya hai…" (a common phrase, not a lyric). The
  audience reflex is "wah wah". Then 2-3 **original** couplets about an editor's life (client revisions, 3 AM renders,
  the cousin's free wedding edit) revealed as the house serif-italic flame keyword over a warm 3D world. A twist
  punchline drives sends ("yeh us client ko bhejo jo 'bas chhota sa change' bolta hai").
- **Why:** "Arz Kiya Hai" is #1-2 on Spotify India and top 10 in Pakistan (M1-M3), so the phrase is primed in both
  countries. Poetry is the most shared culture across the border (§5, S3, Iqbal Day 9 Nov, C2). Jawad's house style
  *is* a glowing serif keyword (brand skill). Relatable payoff = **sends** (A1). Works with or without the song: Jawad can lay
  "Arz Kiya Hai", "Alfaaz" or "Khat" under it in-app.
- **Where:** hook = "Arz kiya hai…" + a visual "wah" beat (0-2 s); middle = couplets (one world per couplet,
  match-cut between them); end = punchline couplet + send trigger + card. A Trial Reel variant opens on the punchline.
- **Build (toolkit):** `J.HouseTitle(caps, key)` / `T.render(word, 'jw_key', px=150-210)` with `T.Glyphs(..., 'jw_body').rise`
  / `typewriter` for the grotesk line; `J.underline(...).draw` per couplet; `K.light_leak` (soft, no full-frame flash)
  and `K.god_rays` on the "wah" beat; `J.embers()` + `K.Particles` kept off type; new 3D props (mic stand, diya-like
  practical light, film reel) from `assets3d_jawad.py`; cues `shimmer`, `impact_soft`, `sub_drop` under the punchline;
  stems from `audio.py`. Keep imagery ≥ 50 % of frame to avoid "mostly text" (A3).
- **Risk:** original couplets must scan well in Urdu/Hindi meter (needs a native-ear pass by hinglish-scriptwriter),
  or it's cringe. Don't quote or misattribute real poets (Jaun Elia and Iqbal lines are widely misattributed) or song
  lyrics. Song-led versions depend on Jawad's in-app snippet choice. A text-heavy reel loses reach.
- **Re-check after:** 2026-12-08 (song chart status by 2026-10-22).

### R3: One-man, two-character desi sketch: suit-Jawad (client) vs streetwear-Jawad (editor), universal angle
- **What:** a semi-faceless sketch built from the two character sheets: the client (suit: neutral, smirk, confused,
  hand-on-chest) vs the editor (streetwear: cocky smirk, sunglasses, chin-up). Escalating "bas chhota sa change"
  revisions (a revision counter v1 → v7 "final_final"), told so **every desi viewer** recognises the "5 minute ka kaam"
  relative or boss. The twist ending flips power (the editor's taste wins, or the client admits the first cut was best).
- **Why:** multi-character debate is rising (F4). "X vs Y" is the biggest Indian Reels shape (F11: 887M). Workplace
  and manager skits are peaking (F8 9.9M, F9). Comedy/memes engage 39 % of Indian users (A15). One-performer sketches are
  proven at scale (`niche_competitors.md` P5). Insider-only client jokes underperform (P8), hence the universal angle.
  Sends trigger: "send to your client / boss / that relative".
- **Where:** whole timeline; hook = the client's first line + Jawad's deadpan (0-2 s); re-hooks = each counter jump; end
  = twist + counter freeze + card.
- **Build (toolkit):** face-compositor 2.5D cut-outs + rim light (`faces25d.py`) on `K.background('jw_*')`; chat and
  feedback bubbles via `ui.toast(title, sub, look=...)` on planes; `T.Counter(..., prefix='v')` for the revision
  number (pass `prefix`, never the defaults) with `K.Track` + `slot_tick`; `K.shake` / `K.spring` on punchlines;
  cues `pop`, `toast_chime`, `ui_tick`, `glitch_short`, `impact_big` on the twist.
- **Risk:** humour timing in Hinglish is unforgiving (needs native writing and a viral-strategist red-team). Mocking
  clients can alienate the people who hire him, so keep it affectionate with the editor also self-roasting. Expressions
  are limited to the sheet poses. AI-generated likeness may trigger AI labels (open question).
- **Re-check after:** 2027-01-08.
