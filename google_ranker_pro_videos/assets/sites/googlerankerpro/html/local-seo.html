Is Local SEO right for my business?

It is worth considering when customers search for your services in a particular town, city or area. We would first review how your business operates, where your customers are and whether a Business Profile is appropriate. Online-only businesses need a different approach.

Can you guarantee a top Google Maps position?

No. Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. We focus on the work that can be reviewed and improved, not guaranteed positions or special access to Google.

What if I work from home or visit customers?

Your profile should reflect how you actually serve customers. For a service-area business that does not receive customers at its address, Google says the address should not be displayed publicly. We would review the setup and the real areas you serve.

How long will it take to see progress?

That depends on your starting point, competition, website, profile and agreed scope. We would establish a baseline and review progress over time. We do not promise a particular number of leads or a ranking by a fixed date.

What does the free audit start with?

We start with your business, target area, public profile and website. It is an enquiry for a focused review, not an automatic scan. Share a website or business name and the places you serve so we can discuss the relevant next steps.

Is advertising spend included?

The Local SEO guide scope is for organic local-search work. Paid Google Ads or Meta Ads need a separate scope and advertising budget. Fees, any additional work and commercial terms should be agreed before you proceed.
