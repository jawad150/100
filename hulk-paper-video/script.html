Hulk Marketing: Animated Video Concept

"The Pop-Up Book" · 30 seconds · paper-craft stop-motion style animation · 9:16 (with 1:1 and 16:9 cuts)

What We Saw on Pinterest

  • Paper cut-out and stop-motion animation boards are full of layered paper landscapes, pop-up scenes, paper cities and hands moving pieces into frame. It feels handmade and warm, which makes people stop scrolling.
  • Brands use the style to tell a journey or process (Moleskine and Toyota are well-known examples) because each layer of paper can reveal the next step.
  • Clean versions use a dark background with cream paper and one accent colour, which fits Hulk's forest, cream and green perfectly.
  • Almost no marketing agencies use paper craft; most stick to flat vector or UI animation, so this stands apart.

The Idea

A business's growth told as a pop-up book. A hand opens a green book, and each page pops up a paper scene showing one step of Hulk's real working process, taken word for word from the "Good work starts with a good process" section of the website:

  1. Get to the real brief.
  2. Make the thinking tangible.
  3. Build. Refine. Release.
  4. Keep making it better.

A tiny paper shop grows page by page, from a quiet little shopfront to a busy, thriving business. On the last page the book closes and the cover shows the Hulk Marketing logo embossed in green: "Your move. Let's make it matter." (the website's own closing line).

The Hook (first 2 seconds)

Visual: A hand drops a thick green book onto a desk with a thud and flips it open.

On-screen text: Every business has a story. Most never get past page one.

Alternative hooks to test:

  • "Your business deserves a better next chapter."
  • "This is how we turn a small shop into a brand."

Who It's For

  • Ambitious UK small business owners and founders who want to grow but don't know what the process of working with an agency looks like.
  • It builds trust by showing exactly how Hulk works, step by step, before they get in touch.

Style

  • Handmade paper-craft look: layered cut paper with real depth and soft shadows, visible paper texture, pop-up folds and pull tabs.
  • Stop-motion feel: animated "on twos" (12 frames per second) with tiny natural wobbles so it feels physically made.
  • Colours: forest green desk and background (#111A16), cream paper for most pieces (#F4F2E9), ink lines (#20392E), Hulk green (#9DEF69) for the book cover and key highlights only.
  • Type: chapter titles printed on paper strips in Inter Tight; small labels in Hanken Grotesk. No handwritten fonts.
  • A real-looking hand turns the pages, which helps the handmade illusion.

Scene by Scene

Scene 1 · 0:00–0:03 · The book (hook)

Visual: Top-down view of a dark desk. A hand drops a green hardback book with a thud and flips it open. A tiny, plain paper shopfront pops up, quiet and a little lonely.

On-screen text: Every business has a story. Most never get past page one.

Sound: Book thud, paper rustle, the soft creak of a pop-up unfolding.

Scene 2 · 0:03–0:09 · Chapter 1: Get to the real brief

Visual: The hand turns the page. A paper magnifying glass slides across the little shop on a pull tab, revealing hidden layers underneath: who the customers are, what makes the shop special, where it wants to go. Small paper notes fold out of the ground.

On-screen text: 01 · Get to the real brief.

Sound: Page turn, a pull-tab slide, paper notes flicking open.

Scene 3 · 0:09–0:15 · Chapter 2: Make the thinking tangible

Visual: Next page. Rough pencil sketches on paper lift off the page and fold themselves into a finished brand: a new shop sign with a logo, colour swatches fanning out, a paper phone showing a fresh website.

On-screen text: 02 · Make the thinking tangible.

Sound: Pencil scratch, paper folding with a satisfying snap.

Scene 4 · 0:15–0:21 · Chapter 3: Build. Refine. Release.

Visual: Next page: a full paper high street pops up. The newly branded shop sits in the middle. A green paper map pin springs up above it, a paper billboard flips to show its ad, and tiny paper people on sliding strips start walking towards the door.

On-screen text: 03 · Build. Refine. Release.

Sound: Three quick pops (pin, billboard, people), then footsteps on paper.

Scene 5 · 0:21–0:25 · Chapter 4: Keep making it better

Visual: Last page. The shop has grown into a bigger, busier store with a queue outside. Behind it, paper hills rise layer by layer in a gentle upward curve, and a small green flag pops up on the highest one.

On-screen text: 04 · Keep making it better.

Sound: Layered paper sliding up, a cheerful little "ding" on the flag.

Scene 6 · 0:25–0:30 · The cover (call to action)

Visual: The hand closes the book. The camera moves in on the green cover, where the HULK MARKETING logo is embossed. Underneath, a paper label slides out with the call to action.

On-screen text: Your move. Let's make it matter. · Get your free audit ↗ · hulkmarketing.co.uk

Sound: The book closing with a soft thud, a final warm music note.

Production Notes

  • Two ways to make it: (a) real stop motion with cut paper and a camera on a rig, or (b) digital paper craft in After Effects, Cinema 4D or Blender with scanned paper textures and real shadows. Option (b) is faster and easier to change.
  • Keep every scene to one main pop-up action so it reads quickly on a phone.
  • Chapter titles must be large and readable on mobile; keep them on screen for at least 2 seconds each.
  • No real third-party logos on the paper billboard or map pin; keep them generic.
  • Cutdown (15 seconds): the hook, Chapters 1 and 3 only, then the cover.
