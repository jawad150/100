## What this video is

- **Concept.** A weekly 30-second tip from Hassan, filmed on his own phone on a little desk tripod: the same desk, framing and jumper every week, so it reads as a real habit. - **The tip.** Find a search with lots of impressions sitting around position ten, then fix the page you already have (title, local proof, links from your other pages) instead of writing a new blog.
- **Why this brand.** Golden Line is the one client where the agency credit is verified (live footer "Made with ❤ by Hulk Marketing Ltd"), and its row 5 is a textbook "edge of page one" case: shown 665 times at an average position of 10.7 with a 0.9% click-through. **How to record a memo (send this to Hassan)**
- A quiet room with soft furnishings, such as a lounge or bedroom (not a kitchen or stairwell). No TV, radio, fan or music.
- Hold the phone about **30 cm** from your mouth, slightly to one side so your breath doesn't pop.
- Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1 or 48 kHz recorder), mono. Peaks around −6 to −3 dBFS. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- **One line per file:** three takes in the same file with a 2-second gap between them. We keep the best take, trimmed to the line plus 0.3 s, under the file name in the table.
- Say it the way you'd explain it to a client across the desk, not as if reading.
- Send full-size files by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Not WhatsApp.**
- His accent is never described in any prompt. The memo carries the voice.
