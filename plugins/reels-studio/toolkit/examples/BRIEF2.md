## ANIM 1 · "DAY IN THE LIFE": what fostering really looks like · `anim1.py` · ≈21 s · 100 BPM (beat = 0.6 s)

**Look: EDITORIAL PAPER.** The client's style reference (an Instagram reel) is bold kinetic typography on a **warm crumpled-paper texture** with deep-coloured type and small "< >" bracket accents.
- **Paper:** ivory/cream. Lighting moves across it, so the creases catch the light.
- **Type:** PLUM `#5B174F` and MAGENTA `#B7006E`. Accents are ORANGE and LEAF.
- **Props:** glossy 3D "toy" objects drop onto the paper with soft contact shadows, like objects on a desk.
- **Time of day:** a gentle cycle. Morning light raking across the paper becomes warm golden afternoon, then an evening glow with a lit window for the finale.
- **Not glassy.** This must look clearly different from the earlier glass-gradient reels.
- **Type styles:**
  - Nunito Black for the big caps lines.
  - Poppins SemiBold for the supporting lines.
  - Caveat Bold, a handwritten accent with a scribble underline that draws on, for "It's often found in the everyday." and "Could you make room?"
- **Kinetic language:** masked line reveals (lines rise out of a slot), per-word drops with overshoot, stacked lists, bracket "< >" frames that snap around a key word, and paper-fold or slide transitions between frames. Add light sweeps across the type and a subtle 3D extrusion or emboss, as if the ink is pressed into the paper. Text must stay razor sharp and legible.

**Copy and storyboard.** The doc's four frames are below. Keep each frame's text exactly.
1. **Frame 1, 0.0–5.4 s.**
   - **Text:** "WHAT DOES / FOSTERING / REALLY LOOK LIKE?", then "It's often found in the everyday."
   - **Hook (0–1.8 s):** the lines punch in fast on the beat.
   - "FOSTERING" gets the "< >" brackets and a magenta highlight swipe.
   - Morning light streak.
   - The handwritten line writes on.
2. **Frame 2, 5.4–10.2 s.**
   - **Text:** "SCHOOL RUNS." · "HOMEWORK." · "BAKING." Each slams on a beat, about 1.6 s apiece, and they stack as a list.
   - **SCHOOL RUNS.** A 3D backpack drops in, and a little 3D school bus drives along a dashed path drawn across the paper.
   - **HOMEWORK.** A 3D open book and pencil; the pencil scribbles a line.
   - **BAKING.** A 3D mixing bowl and a cupcake, with a puff of flour particles.
3. **Frame 3, 10.2–15.6 s.**
   - **Text:** "SMALL MOMENTS / CAN HELP BUILD / STABILITY.", then three supporting lines that tick in, each with a small 3D icon: "A consistent home." (house), "A familiar routine." (alarm clock) and "Someone who is there." (adult and child figures, or a heart).
   - **Visual metaphor:** the frame-2 props morph or tumble into **3D building blocks** that stack into a steady tower ("build stability").
4. **Frame 4, 15.6–21 s.**
   - **Text:** "FOSTERING / HAPPENS IN THE / EVERYDAY.", then the handwritten "Could you make room?".
   - Warm evening light, and a 3D house whose window lights up as "Could you make room?" appears.
   - **End card:** `logo_full.png`, plus a small CTA "Start your enquiry · 0161 241 1332 · organicfostering.co.uk". Hold it settled for at least 1.6 s.

**SFX:** paper rustle and slides, soft type thumps, a pencil scribble, a bus pass (short and friendly), a backpack thud, a bowl and whisk clink with a flour puff, an oven-timer ding, block stacks clacking, a clock tick, a light-switch click on the window, a warm end shimmer, and a subtle room-tone bed.

## ANIM 4 · "£447.60: BUT WHAT IS IT FOR?" · `anim4.py` · ≈25 s · 120 BPM (beat = 0.5 s)

**Look: CLEAN LIGHT SaaS + FOLLOW THE MONEY.**
- **Background:** clean white/ivory with a faint dot grid and soft lavender/peach depth glows.
- **Elements:** crisp glass cards and glossy 3D household icons.
- **The money:** a glowing gold **coin stream** (3D £ coins plus a light-trail ribbon) that literally *travels* through the whole video.
- **Camera:** one continuous world. The camera tracks the stream from the number to each "station" (home → food → clothes and school → travel and activities → the child), so it feels like a journey, not an infographic.
- Distinct from Reel 2: that was dark amber; this is bright, airy and editorial-clean.
- **Type:** the hero number is extruded 3D in gold, AMBER→ORANGE with a light sweep. Headlines are Nunito Black in INK/PLUM; captions are Poppins.

**Copy and storyboard.** Times are approximate and should sit on the beat grid.
1. **Hook, 0–3 s.** Use the doc's recommended "even stronger opening".
   - "£447.60" slams in, then "per week".
   - "Where does it go?" lands with a 3D "?" pop.
   - The amount instantly bursts into five category chips radiating out: "🏠 Home | 🍎 Food | 👕 Essentials | 🚌 Travel | 📚 School". Draw them as small 3D or vector icons, not emoji glyphs. They snap back.
2. **3–6 s.** The £447.60 breaks apart into small animated coins and light lines that move across the screen.
   - **Text:** "Fostering payments help cover the costs of caring for a child."
   - The first part of the stream flows toward a house.
3. **6–9 s, Home.** The 3D house appears, and a bedroom, bedding and household essentials pop in.
   - **Text:** "A safe, comfortable home"
   - The money keeps flowing.
4. **9–12 s, Food.** Apple → sandwich → plate. A 3D shopping basket fills up.
   - **Text:** "Food & everyday essentials"
5. **12–15 s, Clothes and personal needs.** T-shirt → trainer → backpack, and the items slide into the backpack.
   - **Text:** "Clothes, school items & personal needs"
6. **15–18 s, Activities and travel.** A bus drives past, followed by a bouncing football and art supplies (a paint palette and brush).
   - **Text:** "Travel, activities & experiences"
7. **18–21 s, The bigger picture.** All the icons gather around a simple child silhouette (`child_figure`, the orange child from the logo, in 3D).
   - **Text:** "It's about supporting everyday life."
   - Then everything fades except the child.
8. **Final, 21–25.5 s.**
   - The original number returns as "£447.60/week", with a small "per child · ages 0–4" beneath it.
   - **Text:** "Fostering payments are there to help cover the costs of caring for a child."
   - **CTA:** "Want to understand fostering payments?" / "Start the conversation with Organic Fostering." Use a brand gradient pill and a cursor click, then the logo and "0161 241 1332 · organicfostering.co.uk".
   - **Footer (≥ 28 px, legible):** "Weekly allowance for one child aged 0–4, based on current published rates. Rates may vary; terms apply." The figure is verified against organicfostering.co.uk: £447.60/week for ages 0–4.
   - Hold the settled end at least 1.5 s.

**SFX:** a number slam with coin shimmer, a "?" pop, a chip burst, a coin cascade and clinks, money-stream whooshes, a door/house pop, a basket rustle and item drops, fabric swishes and a zip, a bus pass, a ball bounce, a paint dab, gathering whooshes, a soft end chime, a CTA click, and a light airy bed.
