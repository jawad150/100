### 1b. Review-source warning (important for compliance)
The live home-page widget ("Google 4.9 ★, 202 Reviews", "Powered by Google Ranker Pro") loads from the onlinereviews.tech API, seat `662ae7af39ad40bf1d9e1983`. That seat is named **"Global Cars 247"**. Its only Google integration is **"Global cars 247 (268 Uxbridge Road)"**, which is a Slough business (globalcars247.co.uk, 268 Uxbridge Rd, Slough SL2 5NY), with 202 reviews averaging 4.9. The newest review shown on the Coventry site literally says "Really pleased with the service from Globalcars247!" (Lisa Woolford, 13 Oct 2026).
**So the 4.9 / 202 figure is not Coventry Airport Taxis 247's own Google rating.** Do not use "4.9 stars / 202 reviews" in any Coventry video. Separately, the agency should fix the widget or relabel it as a group rating, because under the CMA/DMCC Act 2024 rules on misleading reviews this is a live risk. The "4.6 / 100+ reviews" in the static block also has no source I can verify.
