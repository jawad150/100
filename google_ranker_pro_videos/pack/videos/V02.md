## What this video is

- **Concept.** After the last airport run, the owner sits down at his own kitchen table, pulls the laptop in and sees his three months of Google numbers for the first time. T1's **real recorded voice** talks him through it from the laptop speaker. One person on camera; his reactions are cut to the exact moments each number appears.
- **Hook.** 16:9: the keys clatter on the table, he pulls the laptop in, and T1's voice comes out of the speaker: "Right, so this is your last three months on Google." 9:16 duet: the real totals tile (`224 clicks · 15.9K times shown`) is on screen in the top half by 0.8 s; his face leans in below.
- **Why this brand.** Coventry Airport Taxis 247 has a clean, attributed Search Console screenshot with honest airport-search rows: one search averaging page one (7.0), one shown 607 times that is still at 22.0. That lets the film be candid about what's working and what's next.
- **What the edit reveals.** For the first 26 s the data is seen only on his laptop (our real screenshots keyed into the green screen). At about 26 s (59% of the 44 s film) the first receipt goes full frame: `coventry airport taxi · avg 7.0`, page one on average. His own approved line follows. The close is honest: `airport transfers coventry` was shown 607 times but averages 22.0, "so that's the next one".
- **Framing.** "First look at three months", never growth. There is no comparison period (gate G5).

**Deviations from the plan (one line each):**
- The 16:9 runs about 44 s, not 40 s: the receipts and T1's last line need readable holds, and BR04 needs room as the tail. Still inside your 30–45 s format.
- T1's line 4 gains ", on average" ("That's page one, on average"): master_plan §1.5 calls averages of 6–7 "page one on average".
- Row 1's on-screen label reads "the most clicks of any search", not "top search": its average is 13.6 (page two), so "top" could read as a ranking claim.
- In A-01 and V-01 (filmed from behind him) the light line swaps "camera-left" and "camera-right": it is the same bulb and the same window, seen from the other side of the table. Because his face is never seen there, the two colour temperatures meet across the back of his neck and shoulders, not on the face.
- In the reaction shots the plan's GLOW line says "laptop" instead of "phone" (production_rules §E wording for a laptop).
- New, optional: a 6 s extend of A-01 (`v02_A-01x`) to give the edit more clean green-screen time. If you skip it we punch in on A-01 instead.
- New: the voice-only fallback (B2) gets its own IDs (`v02_V-01_16x9`, `v02_V-01_9x16`); the plan names the shots but gave no IDs.
- In the spoken lines the brand is said "Coventry Airport Taxis Twenty-Four Seven" (numbers as words).
- T1's line 3 reads "Most clicks went to airport taxi Coventry: fifteen.", not "Top one's…": that query averages 13.6 (page two), and "top one" is the spoken form of "#1" (G4). `team.md` §7 now carries this wording (and line 4's ", on average").
- In BR04 the LIGHTING clause "soft shadows under the eyes and chin" becomes "soft shadows under the car and the case": no face may be in that shot (as V01's BR03 does).
- The first full-frame receipt (F1) lands at about 26 s, 59% of the 44 s film, a little before the plan's 65–75%: it needs a 2.6 s readable hold before his line at 29 s.

**How to record a memo (send this to both of them):**
- One approved line per memo, three takes in the same file with a 2-second gap between takes. Each take 5–8 s.
- A quiet room with soft furnishings (a lounge or bedroom; **not** the kitchen, even though the film is set there). No TV, radio or music.
- Phone about **30 cm** from the mouth, slightly to one side so the breath doesn't pop.
- Voice Memos on its lossless setting (or any 44.1/48 kHz recorder), mono. If a take sounds distorted, record it again.
- Say it the way you'd say it on the phone to a customer, not read out.
- Send full size by AirDrop, Google Drive or email. **Not WhatsApp.**
- Nobody's accent is ever described in a prompt. The memo carries the voice.
