# SCRIPT · Bijli series #3 · Sirf 36 Shots (@jawad_mp4)

v1, 2026-10-11 · hinglish-scriptwriter + script-hook-writer. Machine copy: `script.json` (same content). Text-led: the
captions ARE the story (no voice). The VO column is an optional layer for a later approved voice (Roman + Devanagari
TTS text, narrator "hum", never "main / meri"); its windows sit inside each caption's window so dropping it in changes
no picture. House spelling (hai, nahi, mein, bohat, bari, sirf, phir, sab); English tech words in English.
`*word*` = the glowing serif-italic keyword (`jw_key`), one per lockup; the other lines are white grotesk
(`jw_caps` unless noted). Lockup line order is top -> bottom; `/` = line break. Frames at 30 fps, 120 BPM (beat 15 f).

## 4. Word budget and checks
- On-screen words: 57 + outro 10 across 32 s of story = 1.8 w/s average; every lockup >= words/3 s (column above).
- VO (if added): 61 words, ~2.6 w/s inside its windows; the payoff line P wants a second, flatter "client" voice or a
  voice-note filter on the same voice; leave 1-4 kHz room in the music under every VO window (SHOTS §B).
- Hindustani middle ground: ginti, intezaar, sambhaali, kaafi, aankhein, achha are daily words on both sides;
  "camera" masculine (pehla / kaunsa). No region, religion, festival or rivalry reference; "Juma" avoided for "3 din".
- Truth: no Jawad facts, no brand names; the numbers are a generic scene (the toast says only `Client`).
- Loop: C16 "Tab 36 kaafi the" sits on the counter at 35; at the seam the wheel winds it to 36 under C1 "Sirf ek photo
  bachi" (the reel restarts the same roll).
