Before you can foster, you'll need to study… with us. No qualification needed.

Training begins during assessment and continues throughout a foster carer's career. Before approval you'll learn about:

1. Why children enter care
2. The role of a foster carer
3. Safeguarding and professional boundaries
4. Identity, culture, equality and inclusion
5. Working with birth families
6. How trauma can influence behaviour
7. Recording, confidentiality and teamwork
